<a name="mastery of shadows">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Mastery of shadows
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  3037
==============================================================================
This skill enhances the player's control over shadows.
By improving their ability to wield darkness, the player gains better control of
their shadow powers in all forms.
It may also make the lurker faster at using shadow magic.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Cash
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 20 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 30 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 40 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 4.
It can be trained up to 70 percent of your skill max at level 5.
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 6.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 7.
==============================================================================

<a name="fan of shadows">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Fan of shadows
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Spell type             :  Area
Average damage         :  HUGE
Damage type            :  Physical
Sp Cost                :  Special
Casting time           :  6 rounds
Affecting skills       :  shadow lore, mastery of shadows
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1537
==============================================================================
Unleash the arcane might of the Fan of Shadows spell, as your very essence melds
with the darkness itself.
From the depths of your own shadow, a whirlwind of lethal shadows swiftly
engulfs the battlefield.
With a single swift motion, you command a cascade of razor-sharp daggers, born
from the essence of your shadow, spinning through the air to strike all enemies
within range.
This maneuver enables you to simultaneously damage multiple foes, making it
ideal for crowded encounters.
Each dagger, an extension of your shadowy essence, slices through defenses,
inflicting significant damage upon its target.

<a name="manipulate aura">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Manipulate aura
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Sp Cost                :  436
Ep Cost                :  436
Hp Cost                :  218
Using time             :  12 rounds
Affecting stats        :  int
Base Experience Cost   :  4812
==============================================================================
This skill allows a lurker to manipulate the aura surrounding their kukri to
cause it to be focused to different types of energy. Thus a lurker may do
different types of damage with their kukri. The types that they can focus their
kukri to are poison, fire, cold, physical, acid, and unholy.

The amount that the aura can be manipulated is directly tied into the lurker's
level of study within the guild.

This skill using time varies somewhat.
==============================================================================
