Watchers Of The Night guild members are taught how to invade the mind of their foe, allowing them to send malevolent nightmares into the thoughts of their foe. Such nightmares can cause the foe's body to physically lock up in horror, their wounds to bleed profusely instead of healing, reduce their physical attack strength in many aspects, and reduce their willpower to take damage. Recurring nightmares can even turn the foe into a shriveling body of fear.</p><pre>
