Druids are priests of Gaia, the main deity in animist</p><p>religion. Gaia is mother Nature, symbol of Earth and </p><p>Sky, the benevolent Deity that rules over all animists.</p><p>Druids are taught minor magic of all kinds, ranging from</p><p>draining on Gaia's power to heal wounds, invoking a minor</p><p>spirit for protection, or using small magic to create a</p><p>poisoned dart to kill an opponent.</p><p>Druids can also learn to tame animals, using both skill</p><p>and magics to summon a very special animal infused with</p><p>the blessings and power of spirits. They can also learn</p><p>expertise on the use of herbs to create magical potions </p><p>and balms. Certain druids have been known to be able to</p><p>learn to master weather handling and call upon the great</p><p>forces of the skies to provide terrible energies.</p><p></p><p></p><pre>

Guild Level            :  Alpha
Skill type             :  Combat
Base Experience Cost   :  459

target when they attempt to hit it. It directly influences the damage that
the player inflicts (if you don't hit, you can't hurt the enemy). You can not be
