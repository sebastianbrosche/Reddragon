Evoker Of Lightning guild members are taught the knowledge of electrical energy and how to shape that energy into mighty spells of electric damage. They learn to cast electrifying spells either quickly, efficiently, or widespread. Evokers Of Lightning can also focus their amulet to enhance the potency of those spells.</p><pre>
