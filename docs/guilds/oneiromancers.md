Oneiromancers guild members are taught to invade the mind's of their companions, allowing them to send benevolent dreams into the thoughts of their friends. Such dreams can help aid the recipient by giving them more strength and health, healing, protection, the ability to cast spells extremly fast, and the ability to forsee and quickness to avoid impending danger.</p><pre>
