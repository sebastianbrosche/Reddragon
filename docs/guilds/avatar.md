Avatar guild members are taught to embody most of their Deity's will. They learn several advanced spells to cure their companions' wounds, restore their health, and grant them with powerful regeneration. Avatars also learn the greatest way to bring their friends' back from the dead, as well as how to remove most of the lingering effects death may cause.</p><pre>


<a name="cleanse soul">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Cleanse soul
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  490
Casting time           :  15 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  845
==============================================================================
With this spell, the avatar can cleanse another player's soul, removing all of
the effects that death has had on them. Being skilled in this spell is vital
however, as nasty side effects can occur if attempted by the unskilled.

<a name="heal body">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Heal body
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Healing
Average healing        :  Extremely high
Sp Cost                :  240
Casting time           :  2 rounds
Cooldown time          :  Low
Affecting skills       :  mastery of healing
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  971
==============================================================================
This is one of the most powerful healing spell in the healer's spell book.
On occasion, this spell will create an aura of healing magic around the target,
which will heal them over time. It is known to only happen when the healing
energy created by this spell exceeds the energy required to full heal the
target. This effect never happens twice in a small period of time.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
