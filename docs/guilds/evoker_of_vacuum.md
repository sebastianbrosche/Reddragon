Evoker Of Vacuum guild members are taught the knowledge of air energy and how to shape that energy into mighty spells of asphyxiation damage. They learn to cast suffocating spells either quickly, efficiently, or widespread. Evokers Of Vacuum can also focus their amulet to enhance the potency of those spells.</p><pre>
