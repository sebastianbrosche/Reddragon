Master disrupters specialize in the weakening</p><p>of opponents. In the line of harbinger of anguish,</p><p>they are taught spells that enhances greatly the</p><p>vulnerability of their opponents.</p><p>Due to the way disruption works, it is the most</p><p>efficient when used on very big monsters, while</p><p>it will have similar effects than harbinger spells</p><p>have on less powerful monsters. In order to be</p><p>a truly efficient disrupter, one must first master</p><p>all of harbinger skills and spells.</p><p></p><p></p><pre>

<a name="constricted throat">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Constricted throat
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Impressive
Damage type            :  Asphyxiation
Sp Cost                :  504
Casting time           :  5 rounds
Affecting skills       :  disruption lore, master of vulnerability
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1419
==============================================================================
This spell applies a terrible pressure on the target's throat, making it much
harder for it to breathe. Since the target air supply is already restricted by
that spell, it makes the target very suceptible to asphyxiation attacks.
This spell casting time varies somewhat.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 75 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 3.
