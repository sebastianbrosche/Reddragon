Nether Mage guild members are taught to combine all the elements which they have mastered into magical energy called nether. Nether Mage's studies center on the turning their body into pure nether energy and, in doing so, their body becomes a receptacle for the elementalist's power. All the Nether Mage's spells draw their power not from the elements around their body, but from the nether energy stored inside. Thus, the nether mage must be sure to keep its body charged with nether energy, but too much energy will backlash and kill the Nether Mage. Nether Mages learn a vital controlling spell that will help keep the leathal nether energy under the mage's power. Nether Mage's also learn several powerful offensive spells, how to tune the damage of the nether to a specific type, and the ability to heal damage done to the nether's body. If a Nether Mage loses focus over its body, the energy will absorb into the elementalist's guild ring, where it can later be discharged back into a focused nether body.</p><pre>

<a name="body of nether">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Body of nether
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Spell type             :  Protection
Sp Cost                :  702
Casting time           :  10 rounds
Protection category    :  Major
Average duration       :  Very high
Number of stacks       :  1
Affecting skills       :  mastery of elements
Affecting stats        :  wis, int
Base Experience Cost   :  3075
==============================================================================
With this spell, the nether mage can turn his body into pure elemental nether.
In this form, the mage is able to use his own body as a receptacle for elemental
energies that can be used afterwards for various nether spells. The control of
nether energy is very difficult and a careful nether mage makes a strong point
of always having is control spell up and to avoid returning to his normal body
with too much stored energy. The amount of nether energy that can be stored is
linked to the nether mage status in the nether guild. A nether mage must also be
careful not to have too much elemental energy stored when he goes back to his
normal form, for else the huge amount of energy will explode into the air around
the mage, which might prove quite lethal. Efficiency of this spell depends on
the mage knowledge in the basic bodies and masteries, fire, earth, water and
air.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
