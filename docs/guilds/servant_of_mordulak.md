<a name="sacrifice to mordulak">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Sacrifice to mordulak
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Special
Damage type            :  Unholy
Sp Cost                :  Special
Casting time           :  10 rounds
Affecting skills       :  worship of mordulak
Affecting stats        :  wis, int
Base Experience Cost   :  1056
==============================================================================
Using this ultimate spell, a priest can offer all the blood he collected in his
vessel to Mordulak to gather an enormous amount of unholy energy that will swarm
through the room. The blast so generated will annihilate the target but will
backfire partly at the priest due to the explosion of the the blood in his
magical vessel. Failure of this spell might be very hazardous for the priest
health.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================


<a name="blood fire">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Blood fire
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Very high
Damage type            :  Fire
Sp Cost                :  214
Casting time           :  4 rounds
Affecting skills       :  worship of mordulak
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  845
==============================================================================
Mordulak loves torturing anything with blood in it.  This spell lights the
subject's blood on fire, causing immeasurable pain (and, of course, a good bit
of damage).

As Mordulak's emphasis on selfish is such that he is unwilling to grant this
spell to servants that are helping others (though he is perfectly willing to let
those being helped cast it).  For some odd reason, he equates this with tanking
a party - he presumes the person in front is the primary beneficiary.  Shrug,
Gods, who can explain them?
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================

<a name="appoint acolyte of mordulak">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Appoint acolyte of mordulak
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  468
Casting time           :  1 round
Affecting skills       :  worship of mordulak
Base Experience Cost   :  1014
==============================================================================
A priest of BOLDREDMordulakRESET can appoint acolytes from the general
(non-priestly) population.  Acolytes of BOLDREDMordulakRESET get a blood vessel
of their own, with which to make minor sacrifices to the god.  Once appointed,
an acolyte may not change his or her affiliation without changing his very body.

BOLDREDMordulakRESET's acolytes gain, in small part, the ability to make even
more sacrifices to him, much as his priests have.

As weavers and unravellers have already advanced past the acolyte stage, they
obviously cannot be appointed acolytes.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================
