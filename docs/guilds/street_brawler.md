Street Brawler guild members are taught to improve their brawling skills to help them survive the streets. They learn how to effectively dodge and hit faster, allowing two hits instead of one. They also learn how to fight dirty if they need to.</p><pre>


<a name="tanking">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Tanking
Guild Level            :  Alpha
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  557
==============================================================================
Protecting a party from the blows of their enemies is a hard, thankless task. 
Doing so well takes training and practice.  That's where this skill comes in. 
With this skill, party members may attempt to protect the party members who
stand behind them.  The more people being protected, the more training this
takes.  Obviously, different guilds gain different techniques, and thus they are
not all equally efficient.  Warrior, being the biggest and baddest tanks, are,
of course, unrivaled in their abilities.

Please note that in the case of shapeshifters, ursine forms can make use of the
training in a 'normal' way, whereas canine forms tank better the more of them
that there are. Other shapeshifter forms do not have the ability to tank at all.

Also note that necromancer familiars do need this skill to properly tank as
well.

You feel as though you could safely tank for 10 people.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 5 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 10 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 15 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 20 percent of your skill max at level 4.
It can be trained up to 25 percent of your skill max at level 5.
It can be trained up to 30 percent of your skill max at level 6.
It can be trained up to 35 percent of your skill max at level 7.
It can be trained up to 40 percent of your skill max at level 8.
It can be trained up to 45 percent of your skill max at level 9.
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 10.
==============================================================================

<a name="dodge">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Dodge
Guild Level            :  Beta
Skill type             :  Combat
Base Experience Cost   :  1316
==============================================================================
Dodge trains a fighter to be quick and nimble on his/her feet. With this
skill one can actually dodge combat blows, and helps considerably when 
attacking larger monsters. Effectiveness of that skill is considerably
affected by how much you carry, ie your capacity modifier.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Unknown
==============================================================================


<a name="dirty kick">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Dirty kick
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Under average
Damage type            :  Physical
Ep Cost                :  200
Using time             :  2 rounds
Affecting skills       :  mighty blow, street brawling
Affecting stats        :  str, dex
Base Experience Cost   :  1108
==============================================================================
As street brawlers are often forced to fight in small spaces, they are  trained
in the skills necessary for doing so from the beginning.  They learn a lot of
different techniques, including using one's knees and legs to kick an opponent
in sensitive areas.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================

<a name="tackle">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Tackle
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Extremely low
Damage type            :  Physical
Ep Cost                :  186
Using time             :  3 rounds
Affecting stats        :  dex
Base Experience Cost   :  672
==============================================================================
With this skill a brawler draws on the physical abilities he has learnt as an
acrobat and tackles a target to the ground! This greatly reduced the target
ability to fight, even though the user of the skill has to maintain his tackle
and cannot achieve much while he is tackling his opponent!
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Pavilion, Sigwald
==============================================================================


<a name="street brawling">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Street brawling
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  1312
==============================================================================
This skill reflects all the small tricks and techniques that street brawlers are
taught or learn by practice in order to enhance effects of their fighting
skills. It greatly enhances the abilities of a street brawler to disturb his
opponent in a fight. It also decreases the brawlers chance to be surprised in a
fight, thus allowing them to ignore some stuns.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
