### Hard Rules (Locked)
1. **Shell never exceeds €20,000** for under 100 m²
2. **Labour never exceeds 50% of material cost**
3. **Labour never exceeds €10,000** when shell is €20,000
4. **Primary metric: € per m²** — The goal is a finished house that costs no more per m² than the land it stands on (target: house-to-land ratio of ~6:1, versus 30–50:1 for conventional)
5. **Build time never exceeds 14 active days** for shell
