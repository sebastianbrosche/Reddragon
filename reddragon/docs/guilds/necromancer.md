</p><p>The Necromancers guild is special in some ways and because of this it is a requirement for all to read the guild book located in the necromancers guild hall before you can join the guild. If you are considering to join the guild it would probably be a good idea to read the book before you leave your current one.</p><p>%^RED%^NOTICE: If you lose lich status and / or guild masteries from reincing or otherwise you will NOT be reimbursed in ANY way. If your minions lose worth and / or eq due to bugs or otherwise, you will NOT be reimbursed. Basically you will NOT be reimbursed for anything that can go wrong while playing this guild. Exceptions can be made, but only in rare cases.%^RESET%^</p><p>%^RED%^NOTICE: Your familiars are not safe from death from time of life event, nor in arena, gauntlet, or during battle royale event. In short, there are no situations where your familiar is safe from death. Assume that nothing that prevents you from dying will do the same for them.%^RESET%^</p><p></p><pre>

<a name="animate dead">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Animate dead
Guild Level            :  Beta
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  234
Casting time           :  2 rounds
Affecting skills       :  hematology, mind power, mastery of mummification,
anatomy, voodooism, osteology, dead speak
Affecting stats        :  int
Base Experience Cost   :  431
==============================================================================
This spell enables the necromancer to bring back a corpse to life, or rather,
undeath. The undead creature will, if the necromancer is strong enough, obey the
caster of this spell and assist him in combat. This spell is also in some ways
affected by the skill minion control since if this skill is low and the caster
tries to bring forth a creature that is too powerful the undead might turn on
him and attack him. Besides the intelligence and knowledge of the dead speak
skill of the caster, the power of the undead is also based on the level of the
corpse you use to raise it. 
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Snoop
==============================================================================

<a name="corpse explosion">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Corpse explosion
Guild Level            :  Beta
Spell type             :  Area
Average damage         :  Special
Damage type            :  Physical
Sp Cost                :  Special
Casting time           :  4 rounds
Affecting skills       :  mind power, hematology, osteology, anatomy
Affecting stats        :  int
Base Experience Cost   :  862
==============================================================================
With this spell the necromancer can fill a corpse with an abundance of magical

to the necromancers enemies. Beside from the masteries that affect this spell,

able to hold a greater amount of energy and so the explosion and power of the
blast will be greater.
