Evokers are mages that specialize into</p><p>handling magical energies in a raw form.</p><p>They learn to control various types of</p><p>energies and can use it to shape terrible</p><p>offensive spells.</p><p>All evokers are given a prismatic amulet</p><p>as the symbol of their guild membership.</p><p>The amulet can be charged with magical</p><p>energy with the channel energy skill,</p><p>allowing it to sometimes enhance spells.</p><p>Advanced evokers learn to focus their </p><p>amulet on a particular type of energy</p><p>in order to get better enhancement for</p><p>their spells based on that energy.</p><p></p><p></p><pre>

Help on skill          :  Evocation
Guild Level            :  Beta
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  1142
==============================================================================
This is the skill used by Evokers to control the incredible powers that
they call forth.  Failing to train this skill well enough may result
in losing control of the evoked powers, but mastery of it may increase
the forces evoked.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Onyx
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 20 percent of your skill max at level 16.
It can be trained up to 40 percent of your skill max at level 17.
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 18.
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 19.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 20.
==============================================================================
