<a name="backstab">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Backstab
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Incredible
Damage type            :  Physical
Ep Cost                :  Special
Using time             :  4 rounds
Affecting skills       :  armor piercing, knowledge of pressure points
Affecting stats        :  dex
Base Experience Cost   :  1786
==============================================================================
With this skill, a rogue wielding a piercing weapon jabs it into his opponent
from behind, preferably in a nice, soft spot, doing a large amount of damage.
The target needs to be unaware of the attack for it to succeed. A properly
trained lurker can even instantly kill the target, though the power of the
target and the lurker's own training and previous experience with such tactics
all factor in the prove how frequently this is effective.
This skill is also affected by the skills Double Stab, Triple Stab, Psychotic
Stabbing, Uncanny Stabbing, Bleed and Stun.

The cost to your endurance varies based on how vicious your stabbing is.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald, modified by Cebcurg
==============================================================================
