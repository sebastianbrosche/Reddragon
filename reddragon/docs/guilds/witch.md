Witch guild members are taught the magical art of witchcraft. They learn the basics of offensive witchcraft as well as the ability to charge their broom, which they are bestowed with as a sign of their guild membership. Charged brooms are vital to some of the witch's powers, it can heal the witch and the witch's companions, and it can harm the witch's foes.</p><pre>


<a name="evil eye">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Evil eye
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Impressive
Damage type            :  Psionic
Sp Cost                :  160
Casting time           :  3 rounds
Affecting skills       :  lore of the elders
Affecting stats        :  int
Base Experience Cost   :  795
==============================================================================
With this spell the witch concentrates his or her mental energies literally
behind the targets eyes, causing huge mental pressure to be applied to the
targets brain and making the target bleed internally. The effects of this spell
are rather gruesome to watch, especially since when torturing the target with
the evil eye, the caster stays utterly silent and calm.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sven, modified by Samael and Wyrn
==============================================================================

<a name="remove curse">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Remove curse
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  192
Ep Cost                :  34
Casting time           :  Instant
Affecting skills       :  lore of the elders
Affecting stats        :  int
Base Experience Cost   :  663
==============================================================================
This spell allows the witch who casts it to remove curses from people. While
generally two witches would not want to desecrate the other's fine curse work,
there are times when a witch really just doesn't want to wait for the other's
magic to decay. They've got places to go, things to do, and other people to
curse. In those moments of frustrated impatience, this spell can be quite handy.
Simply cast it at the person who's stupidly gotten himself cursed, and admire
the results. Of course, there are no guarantees in this world, and another
witch's curse could always be powerful enough that you don't get it in the first
try. Or the second. Or the... well, you get the idea.

A witch who has not even mastered the most basic aspects of this spell may find
themselves injured by a screw-up of sufficiently epic propotions. The lesson
here is to learn the basics before you try the tricky stuff.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Vor
