Bard guild members learn how to produce magical effects through song, instead of through mana. Bards spend their life traveling around learning the lores and historical stories of the lands. Based off these stories, they sing songs that are spoken from the heart and work their magic through music. Bard songs can have various effects from better regen, more wisdom, more strength, and more charisma.</p><p></p><pre>


<a name="disillusionment">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Disillusionment
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Ep Cost                :  146
Using time             :  4 rounds
Affecting stats        :  cha, wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1176
==============================================================================
A bard's livelihood is being larger than life and allowing the glamour of
hyperbole to worm its way into the hearts of those who would listen. This is how
they are able to not just encourage the potential in others, but also in
themselves. This requires no small amount of focused perception on the part of
the bard, who must constantly believe their own hype. That being said, there are
times when a bard must settle into a sense of reality for a time, and to do
this, they need to be able to disillusion themselves and come to terms with
their own true nature. This disillusionment has the unique result of removing
the the effects of any bard-based augmentations.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Vor
==============================================================================

<a name="demoralize">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Demoralize
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Ep Cost                :  146
Using time             :  4 rounds
Affecting stats        :  cha, wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1176
==============================================================================
Bards learn a lot of lessons in their travels, and none is quite so important
to master as a well timed disillusioning. Filling people with compliments
and stories that motivate them to do better is almost always in the best
interest of a bard, but there are times when it becomes somewhat necessary
for people to look at the world with a sense of reality. However, it is not
easy to master the art of demoralizing someone without seriously and hurtfully
insulting them, which would only anger them and make the bard look like a jerk.
Thus, it is rather important that the bard not fail to be sincere and calm.
A proper demoralization sounds like nothing more than a painful truth
expressed in a caring tone to a good friend. It is worth nothing that
demoralization is always more successful when it is done by a friend of the
person being demoralized.

It is important to note that while this skill will allow you to disillusion
people, it's not very effective at demoralizing people beyond that point;
bards are, afterall, motivational speakers at the end of the day, and
purposefully making people feel bad about themselves is simply not in the job
description.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Vor
==============================================================================
