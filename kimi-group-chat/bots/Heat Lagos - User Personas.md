PERSONA 1: "MARCUS" , The Local Lifestyle Seeker

Demographics
- Age: 28–40
- Lives: Lagos, Portimão, or nearby Algarve towns
- Works: Local business, remote job, or seasonal tourism work
- Income: €2,500–€4,500/month
- Status: Single or in a relationship, no kids or young kids

Psychographics
- Moved to (or stayed in) the Algarve for quality of life, not career
- Hates big commercial gyms , feels anonymous and soulless
- Wants a "third place" between home and work
- Values community, connection, and belonging
- Interested in more than just lifting weights , wants sauna, ice bath, yoga, recovery
- Active on Instagram, discovers local spots through stories and reels
- Willing to pay more for a premium experience with personality

Pain Points
- Feels isolated in a transient town
- Can't find a gym that feels like a community
- Bored of working out alone with headphones on
- Wants recovery options (sauna, ice bath) but nowhere local offers it properly
- Missing the "tribe" feeling from sports teams or martial arts

Goals
- Build a social circle of like-minded, health-conscious people
- Get fit without it feeling like a chore
- Have a consistent routine that includes recovery
- Feel part of something local and authentic

How Heat Lagos Serves Them
- Community-first gym with regular events, challenges, and socials
- Sauna + ice bath as part of the membership (not extra)
- BJJ and yoga classes included , variety keeps it interesting
- Local owner (Sebastian) who actually shows up and knows your name
- WhatsApp group / Telegram community for members

Content That Speaks to Marcus
- Member spotlights and transformation stories
- "Day in the life" content showing the social side
- Sauna/ice bath rituals and benefits
- Behind-the-scenes of gym life , the human, messy, real stuff
- Events and challenges , "join the tribe"

PERSONA 2: "ELENA" , The Remote Worker / Digital Nomad

Demographics
- Age: 30–45
- Lives: Temporarily in Algarve (3–12 months) or visits regularly
- Works: Tech, design, marketing, consulting , fully remote
- Income: €4,000–€8,000+/month
- Status: Single or partnered, no kids

Psychographics
- Moved to Portugal for sun, surf, and lower cost of living
- Works from coworking spaces or cafes
- Values health and fitness but struggles with routine while traveling
- Socially curious , wants to meet people outside of work
- Tech-savvy, books everything online, reads reviews
- Active on Instagram, TikTok, uses Google Maps reviews
- Willing to try new things but needs low friction

Pain Points
- Hard to find a gym that feels welcoming as a newcomer
- Doesn't want a 12-month contract when they might leave in 6 months
- Missing the structured fitness routine they had back home
- Wants to meet people but hates awkward networking events
- Needs flexibility , travels on weekends, irregular schedule

Goals
- Stay fit while living abroad
- Build a social network quickly
- Find a "home base" gym they can rely on
- Experience the local culture beyond tourist traps

How Heat Lagos Serves Them
- Flexible memberships (month-to-month, no long contracts)
- Drop-in friendly with easy online booking
- Community events that are genuinely social, not awkward
- English-speaking environment with international members
- WhatsApp group for coordination and connection

Content That Speaks to Elena
- "New in Lagos? Here's your fitness home"
- Flexible membership options highlighted
- International member community , "meet the tribe"
- Lagos lifestyle content , surf, sun, fitness, community
- Easy online sign-up process demo

What Heat Lagos IS NOT:
- A big commercial gym chain
- Just about aesthetics and vanity
- A tourist trap
- Cheap or discount

- Direct, real, no BS
