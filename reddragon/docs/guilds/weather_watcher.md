Weather Watcher guild members are taught how to control the weather and use its mighty power. While outside, they learn to harness the migthy power of the sun and moon, the flurrying winds, and the darkening clouds. Along with the clouds, weather watchers also learn to control the intense rains and the voltaic lightning that the clouds can generate.</p><pre>


<a name="call clouds">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Call clouds
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  200
Casting time           :  3 rounds
Affecting skills       :  weather sense
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  572
==============================================================================
Using this spell, a druid can summon clouds in a clear sky, in order to be able
to call lightning upon his foes. It is also known that the presence of clouds
will enhance the power of the call lightning spell.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================


<a name="call lightning">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Call lightning
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Impressive
Damage type            :  Electric
Sp Cost                :  146
Casting time           :  3 rounds
Affecting skills       :  weather sense
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  822
==============================================================================
This spell allows the watcher to call upon lightning, summoning a powerful bolt
of lightning upon his unfortunate target. This spell requires a cloudy sky to
function properly, and it is known to be more efficient when its raining.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 2.
==============================================================================

Spell type             :  Protection

<a name="purifying drizzle">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Purifying drizzle
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  456
Casting time           :  8 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  572
==============================================================================
This spell allows the caster to summon a magical purifying drizzle. It will
effectively drain the magic in the whole area, removing most of enchantments and
spells affecting the people present in the place. Both spell and skill training
are necessary to the successful removal of the enchantments, and it helps if the
caster is using a particularly rainy cloud.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================


<a name="clear clouds">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Clear clouds
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  174
Casting time           :  4 rounds
Affecting skills       :  weather sense
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  482

<a name="sunbeam">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Sunbeam
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Very high
Damage type            :  Fire
Sp Cost                :  190
Casting time           :  4 rounds
Affecting skills       :  weather sense
Affecting stats        :  int, wis
Base Experience Cost   :  928
==============================================================================
This spell allows the weather watcher to call for the sun's power and direct
some of the fearsome energy on an opponent to burn it with sunfire. This spell
can be used only during the day, and it needs a quite clear sky to function. It
is common knowledge that the spell is more powerful around noon, when the sun is
at its zenith.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 75 percent of your skill max at level 7.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 8.
==============================================================================
