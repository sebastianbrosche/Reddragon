Voodooist guild members are taught how to harness the harmful power and destruction of death. They learn how to summon carrion crawlers and magically make their enemies limbs rot, weaking their body. They also learn how to weaken their enemies and strengthen their companions to the harmful diseases that can arise during the decay of death. Voodooists learn how to also summon a dedicated minion zombie.</p><p></p><pre>


<a name="minion control">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Minion control
Guild Level            :  Beta
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  1142
==============================================================================
This mastery affects how many, if any, undead familiars the necromancer can keep
under his control before they turn on him. With this skill low there is a chance
that the necromancer rises an undead that is too powerful for him to control. 
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Snoop
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 25 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 55 percent of your skill max at level 5.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 10.
==============================================================================


<a name="circle of protection">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Circle of protection
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  386
Casting time           :  10 rounds
Affecting skills       :  dead speak
Affecting stats        :  wis, int
Base Experience Cost   :  603
==============================================================================
With this spell the caster can create a circle of protective runes that enables
to caster to raise more powerful undeads without risk of getting attacked by
them. The power of the circle is affected not only by the mastery skills listed
above, but also by the casters level and guild masteries. Liches are known to
create more powerful circles than non-liches. 
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Samael and Snoop
==============================================================================


<a name="mind surge">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Mind surge
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  Special
Casting time           :  3 rounds
Affecting skills       :  mind power
Base Experience Cost   :  603
==============================================================================
With this spell the caster can transfer some of his energy to an undead being.
This spell can only be used on undead creatures and the type of effect it has
depends on the target. This spell is known to have better result if the caster
has the spell energy field up. 
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Snoop
==============================================================================

<a name="rotting limbs">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Rotting limbs
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Almost nothing
Damage type            :  Physical
Sp Cost                :  300
Casting time           :  3 rounds
Affecting skills       :  voodooism
Affecting stats        :  int
Base Experience Cost   :  693
==============================================================================

<a name="combine corpses">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Combine corpses
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  386
Casting time           :  6 rounds
Affecting skills       :  anatomy
Base Experience Cost   :  603
==============================================================================
