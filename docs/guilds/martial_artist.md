Martial Artists are fighters who have chosen the path of mastering the art of combat by using their fists, feet, and entire body as a weapon. Martial Artists neglect the use of conventional weapons, believing their body and mind is the only weapon they need. They learn all the basic skills involved in unarmed fighting, including how to land several quick punches with both fists. They also learn how to land powerful, stunning blows and the basics at dodging attacks with quickness and skill. Martial Artist are given a pair of gloves as a symbol of their guild membership.</p><pre>


<a name="attack">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Attack
Guild Level            :  Alpha
Skill type             :  Combat
Base Experience Cost   :  459
==============================================================================
This skill helps determine how likely a player is to successfully hit their
target when they attempt to hit it. It directly influences the damage that
the player inflicts (if you don't hit, you can't hurt the enemy). You can not be
an effective fighter without a lot of training in this skill.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Unknown
==============================================================================


<a name="tanking">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Tanking
Guild Level            :  Alpha
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  557
==============================================================================
Protecting a party from the blows of their enemies is a hard, thankless task. 
Doing so well takes training and practice.  That's where this skill comes in. 
With this skill, party members may attempt to protect the party members who
stand behind them.  The more people being protected, the more training this
takes.  Obviously, different guilds gain different techniques, and thus they are
not all equally efficient.  Warrior, being the biggest and baddest tanks, are,
of course, unrivaled in their abilities.

Please note that in the case of shapeshifters, ursine forms can make use of the
training in a 'normal' way, whereas canine forms tank better the more of them
that there are. Other shapeshifter forms do not have the ability to tank at all.

Also note that necromancer familiars do need this skill to properly tank as
well.

You feel as though you could safely tank for 10 people.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================


<a name="flesh of stone">==============================================================================

Guild Level            :  Alpha
Skill type             :  Protection
Protection category    :  General
Average duration       :  Low
Number of stacks       :  1
Ep Cost                :  162
Using time             :  2 rounds
