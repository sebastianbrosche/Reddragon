Heat Lagos, Investor Business Plan

Version 1.0 | May 2026
Prepared by: Sebastian Brosche, Founder

1. THE OPPORTUNITY IN ONE PARAGRAPH

The Algarve is the fastest-growing wellness destination in Europe. Lagos has 40% foreign residents and attracts over 200,000 visitors each summer. Tourists spend €1.8 billion per year. Digital nomads are moving here in waves. And there is no premium community-focused fitness brand serving this market. No one combines hot yoga, BJJ, sauna, ice bath, and real community under one roof. We built Heat Lagos to fill that gap. Now we need capital and an operations partner to scale it.

---

2. MY STORY, WHY I BUILT THIS

I grew up in Denmark, north of Copenhagen. My father was a football coach. I played professionally for B.93 and FC Copenhagen. Then I discovered BJJ after watching Royce Gracie on UFC. I trained hard, got hurt, and started doing yoga to recover. That became Yoga for BJJ, which grew into a big online brand with courses, apps, and a massive email list.

I moved to Portugal in 2018 to surf and live a different life. During COVID in 2022, I decided to build something real here. Heat Lagos. A gym that is actually a community. CrossFit, BJJ, yoga, sauna, ice bath. The whole thing.

We opened in the middle of a pandemic. Everyone thought I was crazy. We hit 100 members anyway. That is the proof.

---

3. THE PROBLEM

The Algarve has gyms, but it does not have a community.

Locals are stuck with anonymous commercial gyms where nobody knows their name. Tourists and digital nomads want experiences, not treadmill farms. BJJ practitioners in the region drive 30 to 60 minutes to find quality training. Wellness tourists pay premium prices for generic spa packages that feel manufactured.

There is no place that treats fitness as a lifestyle and community as the product. That is the gap.

---

4. THE SOLUTION, THE HEAT ECOSYSTEM

Heat Lagos is not a gym. It is a community built around movement, recovery, and belonging.

What we offer now:
- CrossFit and functional training
- Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) program
- Yoga classes including hot yoga
- Sauna (80°C traditional Finnish)
- Ice bath recovery
- Open gym floor
- Community events, challenges, socials

Current traction:
- ~100 active members
- Opened 2022, grew through COVID
- High retention, word-of-mouth driven
- English-speaking international community
- Flexible memberships including drop-ins

The ecosystem we are building:

Heat Lagos (current) , community gym, proof of concept, 100 members and growing
Heat Algarve (planned) , second location, broader than Lagos
Brasa (planned) , standalone sauna and cold plunge concept
Yoga for BJJ (established) , digital brand, courses, apps, large email list
Yoga Teacher Training Portugal (new) , 200-hour program, website live

Each piece feeds the others. The gym builds the local community. The digital brand brings international awareness. The teacher training creates instructors who become members. The sauna concept adds a recovery revenue stream. Together they form a brand that can scale.

5. THE MARKET

Lagos, Portugal

- 35,000 residents in winter, 200,000+ in summer
- 40% foreign residents, 4x the national average
- One of Europe's top digital nomad destinations
- D8 visa program growing rapidly
- Rent 30% cheaper than Lisbon, attracting remote workers

Algarve Tourism

- Record €1.8 billion revenue in 2025
- 2.49 million wellness tourism trips per year
- Average wellness tourist spend: €1,349 per trip
- Wellness tourism growing at 7.4% CAGR
- Portugal wellness tourism sector: €5.1 billion

The Digital Nomad Factor

Lagos is now a top nomad hub. Remote workers with €4,000 to €8,000 monthly incomes move here for sun, surf, and lower costs. They need:
- Flexible gym memberships, no long contracts
- A community to belong to
- English-speaking environment
- Easy online booking

These are exactly our target members.

6. COMPETITION

Lagos has gyms, but none compete with our positioning.

InLight Yoga , yoga studio, no BJJ, no sauna, no gym
Luma , gym with sauna and ice bath, but no hot yoga, no BJJ, no community focus, no teacher training
Commercial gyms , anonymous, no community, no recovery

No competitor offers: hot yoga + BJJ + sauna + ice bath + community + teacher training + digital brand

That is our moat.

12. RISKS AND MITIGATION

Risk: Seasonal fluctuations (Lagos is quiet in winter)
Mitigation: Year-round local membership base, digital nomads who stay through winter, digital revenue from Yoga for BJJ

Risk: Competition enters the market
Mitigation: First-mover advantage, established community, Sebastian's personal brand, multi-revenue ecosystem

Risk: Sebastian is the face and the driver
Mitigation: Hire gym manager, build systems, document processes, create brand that outlasts founder

Risk: Capital requirements for flagship
Mitigation: Phased approach, prove each phase before next, multiple revenue streams to fund growth

Risk: Economic downturn affecting tourism
Mitigation: Local membership base is the foundation, digital revenue is location-independent
