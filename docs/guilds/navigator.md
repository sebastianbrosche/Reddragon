Navigator guild members are taught the knowledge of mastering space and the ability of teleportation. Navigators learn the fundamentals of summoning their friends to them and relocating to their friends. They also learn how to create the mystical gems called lodestones, which can be used to teleport around the world.</p><pre>

<a name="create navigation stone">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Create navigation stone
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Casting time           :  20 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  904
==============================================================================
This spell will create a small stone capable of transporting the user to the
adventure's guild. The number of times the stone can transport, and the amount
of time that it will retain its power depends greatly on the caster's
intelligence and wisdom, as well as his proficiency in both this spell and
mastery of navigation. This spell will focus all of the caster's energy into a
supplied gem, turning it into a navigation stone.  The size and quality of the
gem affects the power of the resultant lodestone.  It is possible that the most
powerful stones may lead to more varied locations.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Marvin
==============================================================================
