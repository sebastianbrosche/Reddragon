Flogger guild members are taught to master the use of whipping type weapons, both offensively and defensively. Floggers learn how to wield a whip with quickness and finesse. They learn how to wrap their foe up with their whip, keeping the foe from fighting at full force. They also learn how to powerfully lash their foe with a whip.</p><pre>
