Heart stands. The fountain is a monument of sorts, erected by the gods of this world. Inside the trees, you can see pools of water,
part of the Gossamer River captured as homes to water beings and as wishing pools. Water surrounds the fountain, and two huge windows,
created by the water flowing from the top, cover an inner haven to the fountain.
