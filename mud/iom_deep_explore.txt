=== HELP GUILDS ===
======================================================================

Guild structure in Islands of Myth:

    + Level system
        Every time you advance one level you are given what is know as an 'open
        guild level'. This information is displayed in the 'score' command (see
        'commands'). You may use your open guild levels to advance in other
        guilds.

    + Alpha guilds
        There are 14 alpha type guilds. Alpha guilds are the lowest guilds on
        the guild tree and basically define what type of character your going
to
        become. The 14 alpha guilds as of August 13, 2009 are:

        - Warrior
        - Martial artist
        - Acrobat
        - Abjurer
        - Elemental
        - Psychics
        - Evoker
        - Necromancer
        - Weaver
        - Unraveller
        - Druid
        - Shapeshifter
