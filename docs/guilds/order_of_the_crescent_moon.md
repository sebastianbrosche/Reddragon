Order Of The Crescent Moon guild members are taught to use their chi to harness some of the magical energies abundant in the world. Utilizing the chi's magical energies, the martial artist can learn to charge his fists with several elemental powers. They learn to harness the power of fire, ice, and lightning.</p><pre>


<a name="neutralize fists">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Neutralize fists
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Sp Cost                :  336
Ep Cost                :  112
Using time             :  10 rounds
Affecting skills       :  chi polarity
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  961
==============================================================================
Once a martial artist has imbued their fists with elemental energy, there
occasionally comes a point where they need to release said energy. In older,
darker times, this could only be achieved by either releasing all magical energy
so that the elemental energy could not be maintained, or by waiting for the
magic to run its course. However, this new technique allows the thoughtful
martial artist to end their enchantment earlier. Unfortunately, because martial
artists are not great with magic or its removal, this skill takes a while and
does decrease their magical energy quite a bit, as well as causing them to
painfully absorb some of the elemental energy as it is released, if their
training is not up to snuff.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Vor
==============================================================================


<a name="chi polarity">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Chi polarity
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  1796
==============================================================================
This skill improves the martial artists control over his fists using his chi. It
affects the duration and effects of the skill fists of elements. 
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Snoop
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 20 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 40 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 4.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 5.
==============================================================================


<a name="fists of elements">==============================================================================

Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Protection
Protection category    :  General

Number of stacks       :  1
Sp Cost                :  304
Ep Cost                :  162
Using time             :  7 rounds
Affecting skills       :  chi polarity
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  961

his chi. The valid damage types that one can choose from are cold, fire and
electric. Along with wisdom and chi polarity this skill is also affected by the
skills, lightning fists, fire fists and freezing fists.
