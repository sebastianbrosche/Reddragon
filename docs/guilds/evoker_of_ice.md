Evoker Of Ice guild members are taught the knowledge of cold energy and how to shape that energy into mighty spells of cold damage. They learn to cast freezing spells either quickly, efficiently, or widespread. Evokers Of Ice can also focus their amulet to enhance the potency of those spells.</p><pre>
