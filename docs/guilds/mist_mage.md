<a name="mist form">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Mist form
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Protection
Sp Cost                :  678
Casting time           :  10 rounds
Protection category    :  Major
Average duration       :  Impressive
Number of stacks       :  1
Affecting skills       :  mist lore
Affecting stats        :  int, wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1900
==============================================================================
Calling upon the powers of both water and air, a mist mage can turn his body
into a magical mist that will dramatically change his form and abilities. In
that form, the mage cannot carry nor wear anything, but he will also be almost
immune to physical attacks and very resistant to several other types of attacks.
However, when the mist mage leads a party, he needs to firm up the substance of
his mist form in order to protect his companions and the mist body loses some of
its powers, depending on how many companions the mist mage is protecting. Proper
training the spells body of air and body of water is necessary to have full
control of the mist form.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
