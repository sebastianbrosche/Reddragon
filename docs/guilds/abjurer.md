Abjurers are mages who have chosen to study the art of converting magical energy into protective shields. They learn how to create minor shields to protect against almost all of the damage types. They also learn how to enhance stun resistance. Abjurers are given a magical shield as a symbol of their guild membership and to aid them in their protection abilities.</p><pre>

Number of stacks       :  1
Ep Cost                :  116
Using time             :  4 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  328
==============================================================================

the magic of Islands of Myth. Using this skill, the caster performs a mystic
ceremony aimed at currying favor with the gods and improving the chance

the gods and reduces the casters chances of successfully completing his
next spell.
