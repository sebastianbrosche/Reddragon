Bone Mage guild members are taught how to control the bones of their enemies. They learn how to summon skeletal servants and even how to sever the connections of their enemies bones, causing massive pain and immobilization. They also learn how to transform their own body into only bones, allowing faster regeneration.</p><p></p><pre>


<a name="osteology">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Osteology
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  1142
==============================================================================
Osteology is the knowledge of bones. A bone mage must have this skill trained
high if he is to be successful with his bone magic. 
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Snoop
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 10 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 20 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 30 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 40 percent of your skill max at level 4.
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 5.
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 6.
It can be trained up to 70 percent of your skill max at level 7.
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 8.
It can be trained up to 90 percent of your skill max at level 9.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 10.
==============================================================================


<a name="circle of protection">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Circle of protection
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  386
Casting time           :  10 rounds
Affecting skills       :  dead speak
Affecting stats        :  wis, int
Base Experience Cost   :  603
==============================================================================
With this spell the caster can create a circle of protective runes that enables
to caster to raise more powerful undeads without risk of getting attacked by
them. The power of the circle is affected not only by the mastery skills listed
above, but also by the casters level and guild masteries. Liches are known to
create more powerful circles than non-liches. 
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Samael and Snoop
==============================================================================


<a name="minion control">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Minion control
Guild Level            :  Beta
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  1142
==============================================================================
This mastery affects how many, if any, undead familiars the necromancer can keep
under his control before they turn on him. With this skill low there is a chance
that the necromancer rises an undead that is too powerful for him to control. 
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Snoop
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 25 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 55 percent of your skill max at level 5.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 10.
==============================================================================

<a name="regeneration pile">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Regeneration pile
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Protection
Sp Cost                :  174
Casting time           :  3 rounds
Protection category    :  General
Average duration       :  Average
Number of stacks       :  1
Affecting skills       :  osteology
Affecting stats        :  int
Base Experience Cost   :  603
