Shields Of Faith guild members are taught how to protect themselves and others from the unholy forces of evil. They learn many spells that create various protection shields around their friends, as well as themselves. They also learn a spell to keep their dedication and faith strong and true to the holy divineness of their gods.</p><pre>


<a name="benediction">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Benediction
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  1960
==============================================================================
Clerics trained in benediction can call upon their beliefs and the gods to aid
with their blessings and protections. With this skill, a cleric can strengthen
their shields.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Dram
==============================================================================

It can be trained up to 10 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 15 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 20 percent of your skill max at level 4.
It can be trained up to 25 percent of your skill max at level 5.
It can be trained up to 30 percent of your skill max at level 6.
It can be trained up to 35 percent of your skill max at level 7.
It can be trained up to 40 percent of your skill max at level 8.
It can be trained up to 45 percent of your skill max at level 9.
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 10.
==============================================================================


<a name="pilgrims protection">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Pilgrims protection
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Protection
Sp Cost                :  560
Casting time           :  8 rounds
Protection category    :  Minor
Average duration       :  Average
Number of stacks       :  1
Affecting skills       :  benediction
Base Experience Cost   :  887
==============================================================================
The pilgrims of the early years of Islands of Myth sought protection from the
untamed wilds. In the times of anguish, gods to protect the good manifested
across the land. These gods provided the pilgrims with the simplest and most
useful form of shielding. Alignment of the caster can augment the power of the
shield.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Dram
==============================================================================

<a name="dedication">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Dedication
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  2041
==============================================================================
The cleric trains his mind to be totally devoted and concentrated upon his gods,
in order to provide his shields of faith with increased will power that will
allow the shield to protect against stunning blows.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 10 percent of your skill max at level 6.
It can be trained up to 20 percent of your skill max at level 7.
It can be trained up to 30 percent of your skill max at level 8.
It can be trained up to 40 percent of your skill max at level 9.
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 10.
==============================================================================


<a name="holy shield">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Holy shield
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Protection
Sp Cost                :  584
Casting time           :  8 rounds
Protection category    :  Major
Average duration       :  Important
Number of stacks       :  1
Affecting skills       :  dedication, benediction
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1166
==============================================================================
The cleric can create a magical shield to protect the target from being damaged
by unholy magical energy. Alignments of both the cleric and the target play an
important role in the efficiency of the spell. Dedicated clerics will provide
limited stun protection when creating this shield.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================


<a name="armor of life">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Armor of life
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Protection
Sp Cost                :  560
Casting time           :  8 rounds
Protection category    :  Major
Average duration       :  Average
Number of stacks       :  1
Affecting skills       :  dedication, benediction
Base Experience Cost   :  887
==============================================================================
This spell reinforces the power of life of the cleric, giving him courage and
protection to face undead beings. The duration and efficiency are highly
dependant on the skills of the cleric. Clerics with enough dedication will be
granted enhanced willpower to better survive the blows and fright from the
ghastly undeads.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================


<a name="protection from evil">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Protection from evil
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Protection
Sp Cost                :  584
Casting time           :  6 rounds
Protection category    :  Major
Average duration       :  Important
Number of stacks       :  1
Affecting skills       :  dedication, benediction
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1098
==============================================================================
Drawing on the power of his faith, the cleric can create a protective shield
that will make the target of the spell well defended from evil creatures.
Dedicated clerics can grant stun resistance to the shield they create. This
spell tends to be more efficient when the cleric casts it on himself, though
goodly targets will get some protection as well, depending on their alignment.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================


<a name="sacrifice spirit">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Sacrifice spirit
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Protection
Sp Cost                :  608
Casting time           :  7 rounds
Protection category    :  General
Average duration       :  Average
Number of stacks       :  1
Affecting skills       :  benediction
Base Experience Cost   :  929
==============================================================================
This spell links the caster to his ally. The spirit of the caster takes the
place of his target thus allowing him to take a single mortal wound for his
friend. When the cleric takes the pain, he transfers some of his health to the
target.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Dram
