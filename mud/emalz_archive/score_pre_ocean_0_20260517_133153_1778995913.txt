USAGE: combat <arg>
Possible arguments are:
	'status'                    - Current settings.
	'percent'                   - How effective did the hit appear to be.
	'blocks'                    - Shows 2 color blocks. The first block is
	                              how damaged the attacker appears to be.
	                              The latter shows how damaged the target
	                              appears to be.
	'number'                    - Numerical indexing of hit strength. The larger
	                              value, the harder the hit was.
	'reporter'                  - Report the conditions of you and your target
	'silent on'                 - Toggle combat spam off.
	'silent off'                - Toggle combat spam on.
	'silent generic'            - Toggle most fighter spam.
	'silent start use'          - Toggle skill/spell start messages.
	'silent finish use'         - Toggle skill/spell finish messages.
	'silent words'              - Toggle spell words.
	'silent skill action'       - Toggle skill/spell action messages.
	'silent round'              - Toggle 'New Round' messages on and off.
	'silent generic other'      - Toggle most fighter spam from others.
	'silent start use other'    - Toggle skill/spell start messages from others.
	'silent finish use other'   - Toggle skill/spells finish messages from others.
	'silent words other'        - Toggle spell words from others.
	'silent skill action other' - Toggle skill/spell action messages from others.
	'attack'                    - Toggle whether or not you will melee with your target.
	'defend'                    - Toggle whether or not you will dodge your target.
	'tank'                      - Toggle whether or not you will tank for those behind you.
