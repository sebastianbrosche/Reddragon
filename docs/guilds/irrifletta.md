Members of the Irrifletta guild learn to take their knowledge in creating magical shields to a different level by creating shields that will reflect certain types of damage back at the original source of the energy. Though this does not protect the target, it will cause his or her foes to be duly punished for their attacks.</p><p>They learn to master the energy of fire, cold, electrical, acid, poison, asphyxiation, magical, and psionic damage.</p><p></p><pre>
