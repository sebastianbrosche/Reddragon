</p><p></p><p>The high priests of %^MAGENTA%^Lloth%^RESET%^, known as Primates, mix freely among those interested in magic, dispensing aid and wisdom to any using their magic to harm others.  They are frequently found performing rituals with the high priests of the other evil dieties to the detriment of the world around them.</p><p></p><p>While they are all 'primates', there is one Primate with a Captial P, the prime of the order.  This, er, lucky soul, while more favoured of %^MAGENTA%^Lloth%^RESET%^ herself, is distinctly less favoured by his lesser companions, any of whom may challenge him (%^BOLD%^challenge <X> in circle%^RESET%^) when performing a ritual with him.</p><p></p><pre>


<a name="true belief in lloth">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  True belief in lloth
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  3037
==============================================================================
The cult of Lloth is very secretive, so no one really knows what this skill
entails or involves except the initiated.  And if _they_ told you, they'd have
to kill you.  Well, perhaps not _have_ to, but they'd probably do it anyway just
for fun.  It is assumed that the effects are much the same as true belief in the
other gods.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 20 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 30 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 5.
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 7.
It can be trained up to 70 percent of your skill max at level 9.
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 11.
It can be trained up to 90 percent of your skill max at level 13.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 15.
==============================================================================

<a name="ritual of protection">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Ritual of protection
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  1022
Casting time           :  1 round
Affecting skills       :  true belief in mordulak
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1656
==============================================================================
This simplest of rituals provides significant protection to its target from all
manner of good-intentioned creatures.  Of course, any protection granted by
Mordulak will protect from Mordulak's favorite injuries too.

This ritual's effects can be stacked.

Mordulak is, of course, prime in this ritual, and at least one other god must be
represented.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================

<a name="ritual of torture">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Ritual of torture
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  1022
Casting time           :  1 round
Affecting skills       :  true belief in mordulak
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1656
==============================================================================
This ritual causes immense amounts of pain and agony in the target.   Of course,
since this spell embodies Mordulak's ideal of torture, information is just not
an objective - the pain is an end in and of itself.  It will, incidentally, also
cause immeasurable damage too - though if that happens to kill them, that
doesn't necessarily mean their pain has ended.

Mordulak is, of course, the primary god for this ritual, and at least two other
gods must be represented.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================


<a name="draw ritual circle">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Draw ritual circle
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Ep Cost                :  266
Using time             :  1 round
Base Experience Cost   :  1375
==============================================================================
A ritual circle is needed for any of the ritual circle spells.  It should have
one point for each of the evil gods.  Skill in the worship of each god is
helpful in drawing the place in the circle for that god.  High priests are even
more effective at drawing the place of their god, though not inordinately so;
basic worship ability is more important.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================

Spell type             :  Special

Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  1022
Casting time           :  1 round
