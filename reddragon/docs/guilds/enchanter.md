Enchanter guild members are taught how to create enchantments on weapons and armors. They learn how to enchant weapons with size reduction, size increase, more power, and more damage, as well as enchantments that add magical types of damage to the weapon. They learn how to enchant armors with stat increases and magical protections. Enchanters also learn how to detect any enhancements to weapons or armors that are innate in its magical properties.</p><pre>
