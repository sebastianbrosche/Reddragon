
## Hestia Foundation — Labour Philosophy Update (2026-05-30 ~20:45)

### Core Principle: Labour Is Not a Cost
- **User directive:** Labour cost should NOT be part of the house cost calculation. The system is designed for **self-build with no hired contractors**.
- **Analogy:** IKEA assembly / Amish barn-raising / Jehovah's Witness community builds — follow a step-by-step protocol, everything legally permissible done by the owner/community.
- **Conventional labour model rejected:** 2,000–3,000 hours with hired trades at €80–150/hr is bloated and flawed. Target is **200–400 hours** of volunteer/community labour.
