Talisman Magic guild members are taught an increase in their knowledge of telekinesis and witchcraft by tapping into the etheral world with the use of a magical talisman. Masters of Talisman Magic require a magically charged talisman to tap into the etheral world and use those energies present. Masters learn how to step into the etheral world to quickly relocate themselves temporarily, summon a shield to protect the psychic from damage of both the physical and etheral world, fog the mind of its enemies which causes them to forget their abilities for a short time, and even cast offensive spells causing massive psychic or fire damage.</p><pre>
