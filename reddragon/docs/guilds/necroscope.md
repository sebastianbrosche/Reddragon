<a name="dead speak">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Dead speak
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  2284
==============================================================================
This skill enables a necromancer to communicate with the dead. The higher a
necromancer has this skill, the more powerful undeads will he be able to
control. 
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Snoop
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 10 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 30 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 5.
It can be trained up to 70 percent of your skill max at level 7.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 10.
==============================================================================

<a name="recall minion">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Recall minion
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  350
Casting time           :  15 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  603
==============================================================================
With this spell the caster can resummon an undead being that he has 
previously put to sleep. If you already have an undead of that type roaming 
in the world then it will be summoned to your location. 
This spell is also affected by the spells animate vampire, animate zombie, 
animate abomination, animate wraith and animate mummy. 
Syntax                 :  cast recall minion with <type>
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Snoop
==============================================================================


<a name="lethargy of shirija">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Lethargy of shirija
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  350
Casting time           :  15 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  603
==============================================================================
