<a name="evil heart">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Evil heart
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Special
Damage type            :  Poison
Sp Cost                :  274
Casting time           :  5 rounds
Affecting skills       :  worship of lloth
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  845
==============================================================================
Priests of Lloth can pour pure, essential evil directly from their heart to
those of their enemies.  Though this does little to enemies who are evil
themselves, or who care little about good and evil, in targets pure of heart and
unused to such evil, this can cause instant death in the weak in infirm.

Lloth's diabolical evil stops not there, however; if a creature is killed by
means of this spell, the corruption caused inspires the caster and all with him
or her to even greater feats of evil and corruption.
Evil creatures, being already corrupt, of course feel nothing from this spell.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================

<a name="appoint acolyte of lloth">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Appoint acolyte of lloth
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  468
Casting time           :  1 round
Affecting skills       :  worship of lloth
Base Experience Cost   :  1014
==============================================================================
A priest of MAGENTALlothRESET can appoint acolytes from the general
(non-priestly) population.  Acolytes of MAGENTALlothRESET gain special magical
powers when evil - but are similarly punished when good.  Once appointed, an
acolyte may not change his or her affiliation without changing his very body.

As MAGENTALlothRESET is generally a goddess of spellcasters, she grants better
spell casting abilities to her acolytes - if they remain properly evil.  If
not...

As weavers and unravellers have already advanced past the acolyte stage, they
obviously cannot be appointed acolytes.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================
