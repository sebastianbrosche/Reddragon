Thruster guild members are taught to master the use of piercing type weapons. Thrusters learn to wield piercing weapons with perfection, by impaling them into their foes. Thrusters also learn how to spot weak points on their foe's body, as well as to open gaping, bleeding wounds with the flick of a wrist.</p><pre>


<a name="impale">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Impale
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Above average
Damage type            :  Physical
Ep Cost                :  232
Using time             :  3 rounds
Affecting skills       :  wrist flick, spot weak point
Affecting stats        :  str, dex
Base Experience Cost   :  1441
==============================================================================
Using the special properties of his piercing weapon, the fighter can do a
terrible attack by litteraly impaling his opponent with his weapon, tearing
through armor and flesh. With good training, the fighter learn to aim at very

increase the damage of the wound.
