Acrobats are rogues who learn many acrobatic skills that can help them survive the streets. Acrobats rely on being agile and nimble and they learn how how to stand, brawl, and dodge this way. They have also learned a skill from their now-defunct brother guild members, the thieves.</p><pre>


<a name="attack">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Attack
Guild Level            :  Alpha
Skill type             :  Combat
Base Experience Cost   :  459
==============================================================================
This skill helps determine how likely a player is to successfully hit their
target when they attempt to hit it. It directly influences the damage that
the player inflicts (if you don't hit, you can't hurt the enemy). You can not be
an effective fighter without a lot of training in this skill.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Unknown
==============================================================================

Guild Level            :  Beta
Skill type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Under average
Damage type            :  Physical
Ep Cost                :  190
Using time             :  2 rounds
Affecting skills       :  street brawling
Affecting stats        :  str, dex
Base Experience Cost   :  1313
==============================================================================
Acrobats very often get involved in brawls, for their public is not always calm
and understanding. They therefore learn quite early efficient brawling
techniques in order to be able to make their life easier. Trick punch allows the

precision, can knock him senseless for a while.
