Herbalist guild members are taught the knowledge of herbs and the medicinal purposes they have when used in a certain way. They learn about all different kinds of herbs that can be found out in the wild and how to scavenge around to find them. Herbalists also learn to mix the herbs they find together to make medicinal potions and balms.</p><pre>


<a name="mix healing balm">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Mix healing balm
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  386
Casting time           :  10 rounds
Affecting skills       :  mixing lore
Affecting stats        :  wis

<a name="find herbs">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Find herbs
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Ep Cost                :  146
Using time             :  8 rounds
Affecting skills       :  lore of the soil shamans, herb lore
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  571
==============================================================================
This skill allows a learned herbalists to search his surroundings to find useful
herbs, berries or roots that he can use to mix balms or potions. It is known
that the type of terrain impacts on the herbs one can find. Yellow cactus grow
mostly in deserts, edelweis are found in hills, mountains and valleys, dwarf
pinecones in forests, roots of mandrake in swamps and marshes, blue daisies grow
mostly in plains and tundras, willow bark can be found close to rivers and
meadows and the vilbor alga grow both in lakes and oceans.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
