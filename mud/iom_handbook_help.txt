=== HANDBOOK: COMBAT ===
======================================================================
[from Newbie Handbook]
******
Combat
******
Combat is initiated whenever a monster becomes aggressive 
to you. If you are standing in a room with a goblin, you 
can type 'attack goblin' and it will initiate combat for 
you. If you cast an offensive spell or use an offensive
skill, it will also initiate combat. If you are in a party, 
as well, and one of the party members attack or initiate 
the monster in combat, all of the members of the party will 
become aggressive against the monster that has been targetted.
Be sure to 'consider' the monster beforehand, and feel free 
to ask on newbie whether or not you are able to kill the 
monster before attacking. 

You should probably have some sort of equipment before
initiating combat with a monster. (see the 'interaction'
section to learn how to use armor, and the 'directions'
section to find your way to the newbie armor shop.)

An important command you might like to remember is the 
'wimpy' command, you can set it at a percentage 
(ie: 50% is 'wimpy 50') which will then make you flee 
from a monster once your hit points drop below 50% of 
your maximum.

To cast a spell once you have learned it, the syntax is simply 
'cast <spellname> at <target>' in most cases, while some spells 
later on will be just 'cast <spellname>' or you can even type
'cast <spellname> with <something>' however you will be used 
to the syntax by the time you get those spells. 

Skills can be used with the 'use' verb, as a brief example 
'use punch on monster' will initiate the skill 'punch' at the 
monster targetted.

To view more information on spells or skills, type 'help spells', 
'help skills' for a more in depth look at them both.

Please note that if you are using a client that has triggers, 
and you attempt to set up a trigger to automatically attack 
a monster once something has happened, for example:
 if someone else attacks a monster, you attack it too. This 
 is against the rules here, and considered 'botting.'  To 
 view more help on botting, type help botting.

=== HANDBOOK: MOVEMENT ===
======================================================================
[from Newbie Handbook]
********
Movement
********
Movement in Islands of Myth is fairly simple, and depending 
on what client you are using, it can be made even simpler. 
Portal, zMud, and many other MUD clients will allow you to 
use the number pad on the far right side of your keyboard as
a means to explore, 8 being north, 9 being northeast, etc. 

To move on your own, simply type 'n' or 'north' or even 
'go n/north' to move one room north. If you wish to go north 
many times you can also type 'N' or 'X n' where 'X' is the 
number you would like to have it repeat the following
command. For example:

> 5 say hello 
  Will make you say 'hello' five times.

The do command is another effective way to input a bunch 
of different commands all at once, by using the following 
syntax:

> do say hello;say hi;say hello
  Which will make you say 'hello', 'hi', and then 'hello' 
  all at once.

More Help:

  Commands are one letter anachronyms as listed below

    a : Print current page again.
    / : /<string> starts foward search for string.
  h,? : This help, OR ?<string> to start backward search for string.
    d : Toggle scanning direction.
        Changes which direction you page through the file(s).
    b : goto the beginning of the file.
    e : goto the end of the file.
    n : Next file in scan direction if any.
    # : goto line number #
    q : quit
    s : Set or toggle off search string.
        s <string> where <string> is what you're looking for.
        Searches are done in the scan direction and page displayed
          is the one directly following any match made.
        Until you toggle s off, any scan will search for a match.
        s by itself turns off the search parameter

    enter or anything else scans the next page.

=== HELP: COMBAT ===
======================================================================

USAGE: combat <arg>

The combat command is a interface on how you would like combat displayed
to you.

Possible arguments are:

        'status'           - Your current settings for combat.

        'percent'          - How effective did the hit appear to be.
                             The formula for this is not specific, but it
			     is based on how much damage you did and how
			     much of that damage actually hit the victim
 			     'before' the victims armor class and resistances
			     are factored in.

        'blocks'           - Shows 2 color blocks. The first block is
                             how damaged the attacker appears to be.
                             The latter shows how damaged the target
                             appears to be. Note that this setting is 
			     ineffective if you do not have a ANSI terminal.

        'reporter <delay>' - Report the conditions of you and your target
                             every 'delay' seconds. Give no delay to turn
                             reporter off. (min 2 seconds)

=== HELP: ALIGNMENT ===
======================================================================

Alignment on Islands of Myth is used mainly for guilds. Certain
guilds have restrictions on what their alignment can be.
The effects usually vary from spell/skill to spell/skill.

You change your alignment by killing those monsters which are 
opposite of where you want to be for example if you need to be
'good' you need to kill evil monsters.

The levels of alignment from good to evil:
         God Like, Saint, Holy man, Good, Sli. Good, Neutral,
         Sli. Evil, Evil, Very Evil, Demonic, Satanic
