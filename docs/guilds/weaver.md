Weavers are clerics who have chosen to pledge their lives to the deities of goodness and benevolence. Under servitude and worship for their divineness, Weavers strive to manifest their deity's will of happiness and preservation of life. To aid them in this life commitment, Weavers learn several spells to heal their companion's life, endurance, and health. They also learn how to erase the minor remains of a resurrected death. Weavers are given an Ankh as a symbol of their guild membership.</p><pre>
