Healer guild members are taught the art of curing wounds and replenishing endurance. They learn several strong healing and endurance refreshing spells, as well as a spell to remove poison from the blood stream. Healers also learn the basics of resurrecting and reincarnating those who have fallen from life's graces and into the depths of hell.</p><pre>
