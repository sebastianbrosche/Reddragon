Sylvan Protector guild members are taught more spells and skills to aid them in their servitude as a guardian in Gaia's army. They learn several more defensive skills, as well as pathfinding skills. They also learn how to call upon the spirits in nature to enchant their weapons with the defensive power of acid, poison, and lightning.</p><pre>


<a name="memorize location">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Memorize location
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Ep Cost                :  486
Using time             :  3 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  1009
==============================================================================
A trained scout learns to find shortcuts and hidden paths, and can commit a
particular place to his memory, thus allowing him to later try to use the
pathfinding skill to get back to a memorized location. Knowledge in the skill
will determine how many locations the scout can memorize and for how long it
will remember it. There are three possible usages of this skill.
'use memorize location with add <location_name>'
'use memorize location with remove <location_name>'
'use memorize location with list'
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 3.
==============================================================================

<a name="spiritual transfer">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Spiritual transfer
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  2694
==============================================================================
Expert woodsmen are taught to draw on the spirits residing in nature to enchant
the weapons they are able to create. Once the weapons are invested with
spiritual energy, woodsmen can drain on a part of this energy in order to heal
themselves. Type of healing usually depends on what spirit is used, and
efficiency of the healing greatly depends on this skill.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 40 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 6.
It can be trained up to 70 percent of your skill max at level 9.
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 10.
==============================================================================
