Magical Torturer guild members learn the art torture and torment. They learn several attack spells and how to magically create a torture rack to be used on their companions. They also learn how to use simple thumb screws to interrogate their foe for information about themselves.</p><pre>

Damage type            :  Psionic
Sp Cost                :  506
Casting time           :  4 rounds
