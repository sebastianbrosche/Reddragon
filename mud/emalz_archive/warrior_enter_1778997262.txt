Warrior Training Center [exits: out]
 The fabled office of QuinSyndrius turns out to be a large hall filled with training equipment of various sort,
weapons, armor and of course the huge figure of QuinSyndrius himself. Currently the hall is empty but you can almost
hear the clashing of sword on sword of many warriors, past and present, as they strive to be better under the master's
watchful eye.
