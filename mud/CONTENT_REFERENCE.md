## Races (27 total)

### Human
The most common race, and also the more adaptable one. Though less gifted than other races in a specific guild, they are good learners, able to master skills and spells equally. They can be both fierce fighters and learned spellcasters.

- Strength: Average
- Constitution: Average
- HP Max: Average | HP Regen: Average
- Dexterity: Average
- Stamina: Average
- EP Max: Average | EP Regen: Average
- Intelligence: Average
- Wisdom: Average
- SP Max: Average | SP Regen: Average
- Skill cap: 95%
- Spell cap: 95%
- XP rate: 100%
- Height: 5'8" | Mass: 176
- Special: Average charisma

### Elf
Slender and graceful humanoids, with fair faces and slightly pointed ears. A very ancient and highly advanced race with a long history steeped in mystery and magic. Long-lived, almost immortal, living through centuries and seeming ageless to humans.

- Strength: Below Average
- Constitution: Below Average
- HP Max: Average | HP Regen: Average
- Dexterity: Above Average
- Stamina: Poor
- EP Max: Poor | EP Regen: Above Average
- Intelligence: Average
- Wisdom: Very Good
- SP Max: Average | SP Regen: Very Good
- Skill cap: 95%
- Spell cap: 95%
- XP rate: 95%
- Height: 5'2" | Mass: 113
- Special: See in the dark. Learn skills slower than humans. Learn spells about as fast as humans.
