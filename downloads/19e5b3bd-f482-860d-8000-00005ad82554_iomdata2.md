Shapeshifter Notes

I will not give you any help on solving the shapeshifter form quests. I have solved all but green dragon (I have not even attempted it yet), so you can ask me to check if something is bugging or not, and some syntax issues I will help with if you are stumbling. However, several quest solutions will be solved with just a simple syntax help, so do not expect much when it comes to syntax problems, as I will offer as much help as allowed.


Available player forms: dog, wolf, timber wolf, cat, leopard, tiger, falcon, vulture, owl, eagle, black bear, grizzly bear, polar bear, white dragon, green dragon, blue dragon, black dragon and red dragon
Available wizard forms: gryphon and gold dragon
An effective skill combo for most forms is a bite attack followed by three claw attacks, and then repeat
The spell 'caustic cyclone' (the black dragon spell) can special a physical/fire/cold/poison/acid vulnerability on the targets
The spell 'voltaic venting' (the blue dragon spell) can special forget and stun its targets
The skill 'migrate' will allow any bird or dragon form to relocate to a different island:
Gossamer - bittern
Oddworld - warbler
Misty - duck
Hyboria - goose
Blackavar - owl
Emerald - sandpiper
Dark Caverns - kingfisher
Everrest - thrush
Sombre - sparrow
You can look at your collar to see special abilities that your form can use, usually the abilities are skills
You can touch your collar to see important information going on with the shapeshifter guild
With the 'animal husbandry' you can only obtain one better familiar at a time:
Eagle becomes a golden eagle
Bear becomes a polar bear
Wolf becomes a timber wolf
Falcon becomes a peregrine falcon
You can have up to one major level familiar and one minor level familiar from the animal tamer guild:
Major level familiars - bear, eagle, one wolf, or two wolves
Minor level familiars - falcon
In the animal trainer guild, you get the skill 'animal friendship' which allows for the possibility of one more major familiar (allowing a total of up to two major familiars and one minor familiar)
In the dragon lord guild, you get the skill 'animal harmony' which allows for the possibility of one more major familiar (allowing a total of up to three major familiars and one minor familiar)
The skill 'herb gathering' can find one of four types of herbs used for various healing
Cat form gains the skills hide, stealth, and hunting, bonuses to str, dex, con, sta, all eleven dtype resistances, they cannot hold any inventory, and they gain penalties to height, weight, and hunger level
Leopard form gains the skill hunting, bonuses to str, dex, con, sta, all eleven dtype resistances, height, weight, and hunger level, and they can hold things in their inventory
Tiger form gains the skill hunting, bonuses to str, dex, con, sta, all eleven dtype resistances, height, weight, and hunger level and they can hold things in their inventory
Falcon form gains a bonus to the skill motion control and they cannot hold any inventory.
Vulture form gains a bonus to the skill motion control, they gain the ability to eat corpses, and they can hold things in their inventory
Owl form gains the skills hide and stealth, they gain a bonus to the skill motion control, and they cannot hold any inventory
Eagle form gains a bonus to the skill motion control and they cannot hold any inventory
Dog form gains the skill track and they cannot hold any inventory
Wolf form gains the skill track and they can hold things in their inventory
Grizzly bears can hold things in their inventory
Almost all the avian, feline, ursine, and canine forms are needed to gain any dragon forms
Eagle familiar has 100cha and Golden Eagle has 120cha
The spell 'magical growth' can create the following three types of plants:
Green - restores the least amount of hunger
Red and green - restores twice as much as the green plants
Brown - restores three times as much as the green ones

reinc tax and eq sacrifice: Reinc Tax

Reinc Tax Reduction
Tax naturally goes down on an in-game daily basis. It is not based on logged-in time.
Certain equipment and gold can be sacrificed (given) to Eje in Illium's Church to reduce your reincarnation tax as well.
For Eje to accept an item, it usually must have either +8 to atleast one basic stat, +3 to atleast one resistance, +5% to atleast one alpha type spell/skill, or +3% to atleast one bravo type spell/skill.


Gold Sacrifice Tax Reduction Formula
A=B*2^(-G/1000000)

A: Tax after saccing item
B: Tax before saccing item
G: Gold amount


Effects

50,000 gold: Natural daily reduction
1,000,000 gold (tax>.5%): Reduces your tax by half
1,000,000 gold (tax<.5%): Reduces your tax by about .1%
Item worth 0tps: 100,000 gold
Item worth 1tp: 250,000 gold
Item worth 2tps: 500,000 gold
Item worth 3tps: 800,000 gold
Item worth 5tps: 1,600,000 gold
Item worth 7tps: 3,000,000 gold
Item worth 10tps: 4,000,000 gold

Types Of EQ

Regular
Any basic item currently available in-game
Nosave
Any item that will not save on a player's body, in a castle, or on the ground
Magical
Any item that gives some sort of bonus and is currently available in-game
Unique
Special items with only one copy distributed in-game through various methods
Ungettable
Special items that have been changed and the original was not removed -or- special items that have been removed
Random pool
Magical items randomly dropped by random smaller EQ monsters
Random unique
Special magical items dropped by random exp monsters that last about a couple of weeks before disappearing. Items are only useable by the player or players in the party that killed the monster that dropped it. Many items are unique items that are no longer in-game
Random newbie
Magical items dropped by random monsters that are only useable by the newbie player or players in the party that killed the monster that dropped it
Spider silks
Silks are dropped by spiders and driders from the Lloth's Children event and can be made into special magical items. Silks can also be combined to save inventory space
Spider silk EQ
Spider silk items do not give normal stat bonuses. Instead they give specials with varying power, depending on how much silks are used in the item creation. The specials include: randomly drain the wearer's enemies of sp, randomly damage the wearer's enemies, or randomly reduces the wearer's spell casting time
