Shapeshifters learn from their guild master, Morpheus, various natural and unnatural forms; they become experts at changing their shape to one most suited to the task at hand.  This is not intended to be an easy guild to play. There are many pitfalls and traps that can make being a shapeshifter a difficult and dangerous task - and this means real danger, not the occasional chance of death. On the other hand, a bright player can overcome these difficulties to become an expert in a very versatile, and sometimes even quite powerful guild.</p><p></p><p>A little more needs to be said about learning forms.  Morpheus (the shapeshifter guild master) will teach you the various forms he knows if you complete small quests for him - one for each form.  No form quest requires any other forms its form doesn't.  Forms are hierarchically arranged, to some extent, and if you fail a quest, any forms above it in the same tree are also disallowed you until you next reincarnate into the guild.  The lower-level quests, though, should be fairly easy, and there are enough forms that few people should get truly stuck because of this.  Shapeshifter is not, however, a guild for the meek.</p><p></p><p>%^BOLD%^RED%^Don't ask for forms while carrying equipment. If you lose equipment due to a form quest, you will not be reimbursed.%^RESET%^</p><pre>


<a name="attack">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Attack
Guild Level            :  Alpha
Skill type             :  Combat
Base Experience Cost   :  459
==============================================================================
This skill helps determine how likely a player is to successfully hit their
target when they attempt to hit it. It directly influences the damage that
the player inflicts (if you don't hit, you can't hurt the enemy). You can not be
an effective fighter without a lot of training in this skill.

<a name="tanking">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Tanking
Guild Level            :  Alpha
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  557
==============================================================================
Protecting a party from the blows of their enemies is a hard, thankless task. 
Doing so well takes training and practice.  That's where this skill comes in. 
With this skill, party members may attempt to protect the party members who
stand behind them.  The more people being protected, the more training this
takes.  Obviously, different guilds gain different techniques, and thus they are
not all equally efficient.  Warrior, being the biggest and baddest tanks, are,
of course, unrivaled in their abilities.

Please note that in the case of shapeshifters, ursine forms can make use of the
training in a 'normal' way, whereas canine forms tank better the more of them
that there are. Other shapeshifter forms do not have the ability to tank at all.

Also note that necromancer familiars do need this skill to properly tank as
well.

You feel as though you could safely tank for 10 people.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================
