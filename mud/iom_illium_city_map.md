- `##` = buildings/structures (thick walls)
- `=` = bridges/doorways
- `p` = paths/portal area
- `c` = cave/closed area
- `+` = crossroad
- `|` = North-South road
- `/` = Southwest-Northeast road
- `--` = road connectors
- `^` = hills/elevation
- `F` = dense forest (capitalized)
