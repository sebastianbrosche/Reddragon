Cauldron Magic guild members are taught the witchcraft art of potion making with their broom and a magical cauldron, which they must summon. They learn how to fill their cauldron with magical energies as well as how to stir magical potions of physical, fiery, poisonous, magical, or acidic energy. Successfully created potions can be thrown at the feet of the witch's foes, which will explode and cause agony and pain to all those around the potion.</p><pre>

<a name="stir mixture of tremors">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Stir mixture of tremors
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Summon object
Sp Cost                :  148
Casting time           :  4 rounds
Affecting skills       :  brewing lore
Affecting stats        :  int
Base Experience Cost   :  789
==============================================================================
With this spell, the witch creates a potion that will create a powerful
earthquake when thrown in a room. It is imperative that the caster get out of
the room after throwing the potion, or the potion blast will hit him/her as
well.
In addition, more powerful monsters are likely to notice the potion on the
ground and very well might throw it back at the caster.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Wyrn and Samael
==============================================================================
