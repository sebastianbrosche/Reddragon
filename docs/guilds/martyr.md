Martyr guild members dedicate themselves and devote their lives to help keep their companions alive and healthy. They learn several spells that they cast upon all of their companions that can heal their wounds, refresh their depleted endurance, and increase their regeneration. Martyrs also learn many spells that allow them to sacrifice their own lifeforce and soul to keep their companions healed and alive.</p><pre>
