Unravellers are evil clerics who have chosen to pledge their lives to the deities of evilness and malevolence: %^MAGENTA%^Lloth%^RESET%^, %^BOLD%^%^BLACK%^Shirija%^RESET%^, %^RED%^Mordulak%^RESET%^, and %^BOLD%^%^RED%^Talakh%^RESET%^. Under servitude and worship for their divineness, unravellers strive to manifest their deity's will of suffering and termination of life, unravelling all that is happy and good in the world.  Unravellers start off as general worshippers of the evil gods, but must eventually choose a master above all others.  Their abilities will, at that point, depend greatly on who their master is.</p><p></p><pre>

<a name="attack">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Attack
Guild Level            :  Alpha
Skill type             :  Combat
Base Experience Cost   :  459
==============================================================================
This skill helps determine how likely a player is to successfully hit their
target when they attempt to hit it. It directly influences the damage that
the player inflicts (if you don't hit, you can't hurt the enemy). You can not be
an effective fighter without a lot of training in this skill.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Unknown
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 10 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 20 percent of your skill max at level 4.
It can be trained up to 30 percent of your skill max at level 6.
It can be trained up to 40 percent of your skill max at level 7.
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 9.
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 10.
