Bannerman guild members further their knowledge of the bard by teaching them several more songs, that are stronger as well. Most of the songs learned here are combat oriented and they learn how to effectively send their voice over the clammer of combat, ensuring their companions hear the song and feel the full effect of them.</p><pre>
