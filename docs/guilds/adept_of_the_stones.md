Adept Of The Stones guild members are taught how to harness the magic of crystals. They learn how to change gems into power crystals that hold incredible magical energy, as well as how to drain the energy from those crystals and merge the crystals to power a single focus crystal. Utilizing the energy stored in the focus crystal, powerful attack spells can be unleashed.</p><p></p><p>%^BOLD%^%^WHITE%^If you have lost your focus crystal and are still a member of the guild, "talk to atizhel about crystal"%^RESET%^</p><p></p><pre>


<a name="create power crystal">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Create power crystal
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  1872
Casting time           :  20 rounds
Affecting skills       :  crystal binding
Base Experience Cost   :  1352
==============================================================================
A stone magic adept can use this spell to create a power crystal from earth gem.
All the gems in the caster inventory will be merged into a single power crystal.
The more valuable the gems, the better the crystal. Spell percent and knowledge
in crystal binding skill are essential for successful use of gems.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 2.
==============================================================================


<a name="merge power crystals">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Merge power crystals
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  982
Casting time           :  15 rounds
Affecting skills       :  crystal binding
Base Experience Cost   :  1605
==============================================================================
A stone magic adept can use this spell to merge the power crystals he possesses
into his focus crystal, thus enlarging the size of his focus crystal. Efficiency
of this is directly linked to his knowledge in the crystal binding skill and in
his mastery of the spell. Some of the energy released in the merging will be
added to the power stored in the focus crystal.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 2.
==============================================================================


<a name="drain power crystal">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Drain power crystal
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  982
Casting time           :  15 rounds
Affecting skills       :  crystal binding
Base Experience Cost   :  1605
==============================================================================
A stone magic adept can use this spell to drain a power crystal he possesses and
channel the magical energy received from the draining into his focus crystal,
thus charging the focus crystal with raw magical energy that can be used with
the spells gem blast and gem flash. Efficiency of this is directly linked to his
knowledge in the crystal binding skill and in his mastery of the spell.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 2.
==============================================================================


<a name="gem blast">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Gem blast
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Enormous
Damage type            :  Varies
Sp Cost                :  224
Casting time           :  4 rounds
Affecting skills       :  crystal efficiency, focus crystal energy
Affecting stats        :  wis, int
Base Experience Cost   :  2163
==============================================================================
Using the magical energy stored in his focus crystal,the adept of the stones can
unleash a blast of magical energy that is reinforced by his own magic upon his
opponent. Due to the random chaotic nature of the magic power drained from gems,

==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 2.
==============================================================================

Guild Level            :  Bravo
