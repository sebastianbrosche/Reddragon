Elementals are mages who have chosen to study the art of handling magical energy from the natural elements of the world. They learn to control the natural elements of fire, water, earth, and air and to shape them into minor offensive spells. Elementals are given a ring as a symbol of their guild membership.</p><pre>

Help on skill          :  Ceremony
Guild Level            :  Alpha
Skill type             :  Protection
Protection category    :  General
Average duration       :  Extremely low
Number of stacks       :  1
Ep Cost                :  116
Using time             :  4 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  328
==============================================================================
This skill is used by the caster to worship the powerful gods who control
the magic of Islands of Myth. Using this skill, the caster performs a mystic
ceremony aimed at currying favor with the gods and improving the chance

the gods and reduces the casters chances of successfully completing his
next spell.
