Harmer guild members are taught the art of causing pain and death. They learn several strong attack spells and one that causes instant death to those who are already near death. Harmers also learn a spell that grants them protection from the the holy and good forces of the light, as well as a spell to drain an insignificant amount of magical energy into the caster from all the players across the lands.</p><p></p><p>Harmers are worshippers of the evil gods - Lloth, Mordulak, Shirija, and</p><p>Talakh.  Though for now, they have no special powers granted by the gods,</p><p>soon the gods will force them to choose a single god to worship above all</p><p>others, demanding special sacrifices, and granting special abilities </p><p>according to the god.</p><pre>

<a name="unholy prayer">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Unholy prayer
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Protection
Sp Cost                :  420
Casting time           :  8 rounds
Protection category    :  General
Average duration       :  Average
Number of stacks       :  1
Affecting skills       :  unholy essence
Base Experience Cost   :  1097
==============================================================================
This prayer asks a particular god to bless the target.  It must be directed to a
particular god, and each god blesses his or her servants differently.  Of
course, one should not expect the most evil gods' blessings to be without
drawbacks.
Syntax is, "cast unholy prayer at <target> with <god>".

The effects differe, of course, depending on which god is petitioned.

Lloth will moderate losses to those blessed - at the cost of their party.  Lloth
will bless those who aid her priests, but not her priests themselves.

Shirija will provide increased health (with other benefits possible if she
BOLDreallyRESET likes you).

Talakh will moderate, or even reverse, the harmful effects of alcohol upon the
recipient.

Mordulak blessing causes all opponents to "volunteer" blood for the recipient's
collection (and, selfish as he is, will only bless the priest).
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Daneel
==============================================================================

<a name="mana drain">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Mana drain
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Healing
Average healing        :  Very high
Sp Cost                :  244
Casting time           :  30 rounds
Affecting skills       :  unholy essence
Affecting stats        :  wis
