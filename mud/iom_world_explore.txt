  Islands of Myth help

Please specify what you need help on in any of the following ways (without
the quotes):

"faq" : This answers most of your questions in an organized manner.

"spell darkness" : Replace darkness with the spell you want help on. You can
   get a list by looking up "all_spells".

"skill attack" : Same as before, replace attack with the wanted skill. You
   can get a list by looking up "all_skills".

"race human" : Display information for the specified race. You can get a
   list by looking up "all_races".

Or just enter one of the many independent topics. The list is available by
   looking up "all_topics". You can also search the topics with eg. "search
   cheating".

Help topic? [?]: 
Islands of Myth is a MUD - a Multi-User Dungeon. A MUD is an internet
    roleplaying game where players from all over the world log in and play
    interactively together to make their characters more powerful.
Your body glows with magical energy.

As you can see, Islands of Myth is a text-based internet game. Commands are
    sent from you to the computer using text. Feedback comes from the computer
    to you in text as well.
