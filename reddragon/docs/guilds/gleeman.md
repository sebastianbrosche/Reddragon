Gleeman guild members further their knowledge of the bard, but also realize you can't get by on just magical songs. Thus they learn several more skills to enhance their knife throwing abilities. They learn how to throw faster and where to aim  for exceptionally vital organs. Their new songs grant healing and physical strength. As they learn more powerful songs, the songs become longer to sing. Because of this, they also learn how to sing and not lose their concentration and thought while traveling around with their companions.</p><pre>

<a name="funeral march">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Funeral march
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Ep Cost                :  242
Using time             :  Instant
Affecting skills       :  lasting memory
Affecting stats        :  cha
Base Experience Cost   :  1306
==============================================================================
At the death of an opponent, the gleeman can serve the final rites to the body.
This involves placing a gold coin on each eye of the corpse, and singing the
praises of the fallen. Watching the proper return of the spirit to the gods will
lift the spirits of the gleeman and his companions. Also, while doing this the
gleeman tells the tale of the opponent's death, thus adding these deeds to his
memory, allowing himself to gain more prestige in his guild as his song
materials increase.
==============================================================================
