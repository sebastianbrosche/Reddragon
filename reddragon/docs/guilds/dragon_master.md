<a name="dragon tail sweep">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Dragon tail sweep
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Skill type             :  Combat
Base Experience Cost   :  2116
==============================================================================
Part of the Dragon's deadly arsenal of attacks is his extremely agile tail
sweep. While by itself, it doesn't do tremendous damage, it will knock an
opponent off of their feet making it hard for them to fight. Masters of the
Dragon style can imitate this type of attack, gaining full efficiency when they
master fully the soul of the Dragon.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Magneto/Phire
==============================================================================


<a name="nature of the beast">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Nature of the beast
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Skill type             :  Protection
Protection category    :  General
Average duration       :  Average
Number of stacks       :  1
Ep Cost                :  730
Using time             :  4 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  2268
==============================================================================
Not only is the Dragon immune to most physical attacks, but seems to have an
innate resistance to magic too. The Dragon is simply able to survive attacks
that would kill others. The adepts of the Dragon fighting style can focus their
bodies to let themselves be filled with the powers of a Dragon, thus gaining
increased protections against all types of attacks. Mastering 'soul of the
dragon' further enhances this ability, making it a crucial skill for those who
seek to unlock the full potential of the Dragon's resilience.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Phire
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 6.
It can be trained up to 70 percent of your skill max at level 7.
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 8.
It can be trained up to 90 percent of your skill max at level 9.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 10.
==============================================================================
