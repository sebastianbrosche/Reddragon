this void is to select the race you wish to represent in the world of Islands

## THE SIGN

```
This room is where players pick their respective races.
Please keep in mind how you want to play, whether that is a fighter race or are
more inclined to use magic. If you are completely new to Islands of Myth, magic
using races are typically difficult to start out as, you might have more fun in
the early stages of your playing here as a fighter race.

The poster in this room will give you an idea of which races are suited for
which guilds.
You are allowed to change your race at any point in the game, however; there is
a cost associated with doing that.  Make your choice wisely.
There is a very good source of help information available, once you pick your
race type 'faq'

P.S.  If you see a prompt in the lower left hand corner
        of your screen that says --More--(XX%) that means
        the mud is waiting for you to hit enter, before
        showing you more information.  Just hit enter
        when you are through reading that page.
```

### DWARF

```

                                  D w a r f                                 
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dwarves are small heavy-set humanoids who usually sport large beards. 
But never let a Dwarf's size fool you, they are known for their strength 
in character and in battle. They are excellent craftsmen and make the 
best of weaponsmiths due to their keen abilities in manual labor. Dwarves 
are not adepts of magic, even though they have some limited potential.

```
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
                              L i z a r d m a n                              
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lizardmen are savage, semi-aquatic, reptilian humanoids that live
through scavenging, raiding, and in less hostile areas, by fishing
and gathering. Adult lizardmen stand 6-7 feet tall, weighing 200 to
250 pounds. Skin tones range from dark green to grey and brown. They
are scaled and have a flecked appearance. Their tails are 3-4 feet
long and although not prehensile are quite expressive. Males are
nearly impossible to distinguish from females without either asking
or close inspection. About one tribe in ten has evolved to a higher
state and it is these lizardmen that are sometimes seen adventuring.
