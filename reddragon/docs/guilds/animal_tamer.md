<a name="call for falcon">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Call for falcon
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  350
Casting time           :  8 rounds
Affecting skills       :  animal husbandry, harmony with falcon spirits
Base Experience Cost   :  603
==============================================================================
This spell allows an animalist to address the falcon spirits in the search of a
falcon companion that will follow faithfully and help to the full extent of its
abilities. It is known that losing this companion in a fight might put the
spirits in a bad mood, and make them punish the animalist.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 75 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 85 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 3.
==============================================================================
