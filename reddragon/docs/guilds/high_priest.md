High Priest guild members have reached the highest rankings of the clergy in service to the good and holy gods of life. They learn an extremly powerful healing spell that draws on some of the Weaver's endurance instead of draining so much magical energy. High Priests learn to master their faith and belief in all their guilds, as well as a powerful spell to bring their companions back to life from hell. They also learn how to create holy water and use it to sanctify the High Priest's immediate surroundings or use it as a weapon against the evil forces of darkness and the undead.</p><pre>
