Harbinger Of Anguish guild members learn the basics of reversing their protective energies and weaving spells that make their foes more vulnerable to certain types of damage instead. Harbingers learn about all the different types of damage and how to lower the resistances of their foes to those energies.</p><pre>


<a name="high energy focus">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  High energy focus
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  1541
==============================================================================
This skill allows the caster to focus his/her arcane power onto 
a target. All types of offensive spells become much more powerful 
with the training of this skill.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Unknown
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 15 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 30 percent of your skill max at level 4.
It can be trained up to 45 percent of your skill max at level 6.
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 8.
It can be trained up to 75 percent of your skill max at level 10.
==============================================================================
