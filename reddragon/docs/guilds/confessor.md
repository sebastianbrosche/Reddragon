Confessor guild members are taught how to pray to their deities for spiritual blessing. They learn to cast many spells that send powerful prayers to their gods for help and aid. The gods answer prayers for healing, refreshment, salvation, protection, and combat strength.</p><pre>


<a name="prayer for healing">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Prayer for healing
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Healing
Average healing        :  Important
Sp Cost                :  202
Casting time           :  2 rounds
Affecting skills       :  pious words
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  603
==============================================================================
Using this spell the caster can pray for a heal on behalf of any person over any
distance. If the caster manages to receive the gods' attention, they will grant
the gift of a heal to the person who is the target of these prayers. This spell
is more efficient when cast in a consecrated area.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Samael, original distant heal code by DS
==============================================================================

Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Mastery

words in his prayers, making it more likely that the gods
will hear confessor out.
