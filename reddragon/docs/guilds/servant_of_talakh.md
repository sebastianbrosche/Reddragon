<a name="taunt">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Taunt
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Ep Cost                :  292
Using time             :  1 round
Affecting skills       :  worship of talakh
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1009
==============================================================================
With this skill, a worshipper of Talakh can tease and taunt a potential opponent
until they lose control of themselves.  They will then, of course, attack the
priest, but will do so in a rather unintelligent manner.  It is known that the
longer one taunts an opponent, the longer they will be out of control.  To stop
taunting once started, start casting another spell, using another skill, or
simply type 'cast stop'.
