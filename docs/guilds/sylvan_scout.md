Sylvan Scout guild members are taught more spells and skills to aid them in their servitude as a solider in Gaia's army. They learn a several more attack skills, as well as several tracking skills. They also learn how to call upon the spirits in nature to enchant their weapons with the offensive power of acid, poison, and lightning.</p><pre>
