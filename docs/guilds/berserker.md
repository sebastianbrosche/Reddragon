Berserker guild members are taught better control over their own body, to enhance their ability to fight and take damage in combat. Berserkers learn several skills that help reduce the physical damage they receive and help increase their overall strength and dexterity. They also learn how to focus their body to heal itself.</p><pre>

Skill type             :  Mastery

Berserkers are taught to exert a tight control over their bodies, learning all
its strength and weaknesses. By exploiting this knowledge, they can greatly

fighting abilities or ignoring damage in battle..
