Sylvan Ward guild members are taught the neccessary beginging spells and skills to serve as a guardian in Gaia's army. They learn how to make natural shields and how to defend or fight with them. Sylvan wards also learn the basics of calling upon the spirits present in nature to enchant their weapons with the defensive power of the earth or fire.</p><pre>


<a name="weave twigshield">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Weave twigshield
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Sp Cost                :  152
Ep Cost                :  228
Using time             :  12 rounds
Affecting skills       :  natural weapon lore
Affecting stats        :  wis, dex
Base Experience Cost   :  806
==============================================================================
A trained woodsman can learn how to weave a very special shield from a few
branches and twigs. He can fashion a magical shield that will contain great
powers granted by Nature, and that only he can use properly. The shield that the
woodsman creates by skill and magic is extremely solid and resistant. Only one
shield can be created by the woodsman, and it can be used only by him. Guild
knowledge and masteries have noticeable effects on the power of the shield.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Samael and Sigwald
==============================================================================


<a name="inner walls">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Inner walls
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Protection
Protection category    :  Minor
Average duration       :  Above average
Number of stacks       :  1
Ep Cost                :  332
Using time             :  5 rounds
Affecting skills       :  willow roots
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1175
==============================================================================
The learned woodsman can focus his thoughts and inner strength to be in complete
harmony with Nature, thus gaining a better control over his own mind. It has
been said that expert woodsmen were able to ignore some psychic or magical
attacks using these techniques, and it has been long known that woodsmen could
use their mind walls to avoid getting stunned in combat. Expert woodsmen are
also taught the willow roots skill, and knowledge of that skill can improve the
effects of inner walls.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 5.
==============================================================================


<a name="survival reflexes">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Survival reflexes
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  1428
==============================================================================
A trained woodsman can handle his nature's twigshield or his rootstaff with
almost careless speed, relying on his instinct and reflexes to deflect attacks
aimed at him. However, the woodsman lacks the formal training of a warrior and
cannot be as efficient, especially in large parties where he has difficulties to
protect his party members.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 40 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 60 percent of your skill max at level 4.
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 5.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 6.
==============================================================================

<a name="fire warding">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Fire warding
Guild Level            :  Gamma
Spell type             :  Enchantment
Sp Cost                :  408
Ep Cost                :  174
Casting time           :  6 rounds
Protection category    :  Minor
Average duration       :  Extremely high
Number of stacks       :  1
Affecting skills       :  harmony with nature, spiritual transfer, spiritual

Affecting stats        :  wis

Woodsman are taught how to deal with fire. It is as much a friend as an enemy,

great protection from fire for a woodsman's twigshield or rootstaff.

<a name="spiritual transfer">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Spiritual transfer
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  2694
==============================================================================
Expert woodsmen are taught to draw on the spirits residing in nature to enchant
the weapons they are able to create. Once the weapons are invested with

efficiency of the healing greatly depends on this skill.
