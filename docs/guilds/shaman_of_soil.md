Shaman Of Soil guild members are taught how to harness the power of the land, the dirt covering the lands, and the roots that twist their way under the lands. They learn how to travel along the roots to transport themselves around the world. They also learn how to cast attack and defense spells utilizing the rocks and roots abundant in the wilderness. Shamans Of Soil are given a staff as a symbol of their guild membership.</p><pre>

Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Ep Cost                :  350
Using time             :  4 rounds
Affecting skills       :  lore of the soil shamans
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1537

The shaman can plant his staff in the soil and concentrate to charge it with

<a name="soil staff bash">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Soil staff bash
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Important
Damage type            :  Varies
Sp Cost                :  110
Ep Cost                :  60
Using time             :  2 rounds
Affecting skills       :  lore of the soil shamans
Affecting stats        :  str
Base Experience Cost   :  2334
==============================================================================
Shamans learn how to use their staff in a deadly fashion, by bashing their

its full potential.

==============================================================================


<a name="hurl rock">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Hurl rock
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Extremely high
Damage type            :  Physical
Sp Cost                :  256
Casting time           :  5 rounds
Affecting skills       :  lore of the soil shamans
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1056
==============================================================================
The Shaman of Soil can create a rock magically by gathering from all around him
dust and soil particles. He can then hurl that magical creation at his opponent.
Such a rock has an enhanced density due to its magical nature and creates a
terrible impact that is likely to hurt the target very much and possibly stun
it. The shaman can also use the energy stored in his staff to increase the power
of the rock he creates.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================

Average duration       :  Under average
Number of stacks       :  1

<a name="grow personal tree">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Grow personal tree
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Summon object
Sp Cost                :  1754
Casting time           :  30 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  1183
==============================================================================
This spell allow the shaman to grow a magical tree that is his very own. This
magical tree will have a permanent link to the shaman and he will therefore be
able to transport himself back to it through the root system from anywhere in
the Islands of Myth world. Growing the tree involves a big personal investment
from the Shaman, and the tree will be quite permanent unless the world comes to
an end or the Shaman decides to dispel his own tree. Dispelling a tree is
however a very costly process since the Shaman just dispels all the energy he
set in the tree into the air.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
