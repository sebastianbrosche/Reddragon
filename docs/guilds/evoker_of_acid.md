Evoker Of Acid guild members are taught the knowledge of acidic energy and how to shape that energy into mighty spells of acid damage. They learn to cast corrosive spells either quickly, efficiently, or widespread. Evokers Of Acid can also focus their amulet to enhance the potency of those spells.</p><pre>


<a name="acidic ray">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Acidic ray
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  Enormous
Damage type            :  Acid
Sp Cost                :  208
Casting time           :  4 rounds
Affecting skills       :  acid evocation
Affecting stats        :  int
Base Experience Cost   :  2112
==============================================================================
Evokers of Acid specialize in the creation of powerful spell of acid energy. The
evoker summons some acid energy and shape it into a tight ray of acid that he
directs at his opponent. Containing all the energy into the ray is the hard
trick of that spell, but it allows a very efficient use of magical energy.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================

Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  2286
==============================================================================
Evokers of Acid need special knowledge in order to get the best out of the
spells they learn. This skill teaches the evoker all the secret lore needed in
order to become a true master of the evocation of Acid.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 10 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 25 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 3.
It can be trained up to 75 percent of your skill max at level 4.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 5.
==============================================================================
