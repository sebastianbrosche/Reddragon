Master Abjurer guild members do not learn any more protective spells other than weak spells against the rare damage types of holy and unholy. Master Abjurers, instead, take the knowledge of creating protection spells and learn how to make those spells more stronger, more efficient, and last longer. They also learn how to dispel the protective energy shields currently surrounding their friends.</p><pre>

Help on skill          :  Find weakness
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Miscellaneous
Ep Cost                :  506
Using time             :  4 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  1537
==============================================================================
