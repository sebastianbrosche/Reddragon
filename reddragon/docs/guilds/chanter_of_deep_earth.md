Chanter Of Deep Earth guild members are taught how to concentrate on their focus crystal to release a specific type of energy from the crystal. They learn how to focus their crystal to either acid, asphyxiation, cold, electric, fire, magical, physical, or poison damage type.</p><pre>

<a name="crystal attunement">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Crystal attunement
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  2046
Casting time           :  10 rounds
Affecting skills       :  mastery of attunement
Base Experience Cost   :  1656
==============================================================================
A master of the stone magic learn how to link with his focus crystal and attune
it to a special damage type, allowing him to impose some control over the damage
output of the offensive spells of the Stone Magic guild. Proper syntax to cast
this spell is 'cast crystal attunement with <damage_type>'. This spell can also
be used to remove all attunement on the focus crystal by entering 'none' as
wanted damage type.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 80 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 2.
==============================================================================
