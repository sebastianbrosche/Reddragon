Stygian Sorcerer guild members are taught how to transfer their own body's energy to their dedicated servants, strengthening their essence. They learn how to attack their enemies' mind, but the power is reduced the more servants they have summoned as it requires some focus to maintain each servant. They also learn how to summon a dedicated minion wraith.</p><p></p><pre>


<a name="mind power">==============================================================================
Help on skill          :  Mind power
Guild Level            :  Bravo
Skill type             :  Mastery
Base Experience Cost   :  5717
==============================================================================
This skill enhances the necromancers mind. Its primary function is to enhance
the casters ability to transfer energy from himself to his undeads, but it can
also help other spells. 
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Snoop
==============================================================================
It can be trained up to 25 percent of your skill max at level 1.
It can be trained up to 55 percent of your skill max at level 5.
It can be trained up to 75 percent of your skill max at level 7.
==============================================================================

<a name="mind blast">==============================================================================

Guild Level            :  Bravo
Spell type             :  Attack
Average damage         :  High
Damage type            :  Psionic
Sp Cost                :  148
Casting time           :  4 rounds
Affecting skills       :  mind power
Affecting stats        :  int
Base Experience Cost   :  1098

With this spell the caster can send off a powerful blast of psionic energy that
