Guardian guild members are taught the final knowledge of mastering the abjurer guild. Utilizing the ability to tune their guild shield, Guardians can cast both strong and weak protection shields over all of their companions. They also how to cast powerful shields to protect against the rare damage types of holy and unholy.</p><pre>

Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  Special
Casting time           :  12 rounds
Base Experience Cost   :  1360

<a name="wall of force">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Wall of force
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  642
Casting time           :  4 rounds
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1301
==============================================================================
This spell creates a wall of force that can be used by the caster to block an
exit in one direction. Only one wall can be summoned at one place at a time, and
that spell is not compatible with shelter. To properly cast this spell, use the
syntax 'cast wall of force with <direction>.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Sigwald
==============================================================================


<a name="party shield">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Party shield
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Spell type             :  Protection
Sp Cost                :  Special
Casting time           :  10 rounds
Protection category    :  General
Average duration       :  Special
Number of stacks       :  1 for Iron Will, 3 for Aura of Force
Base Experience Cost   :  1360
==============================================================================
This spell allows the abjurer to either protect his or her entire party from
stuns or give his or her entire party magical armor to protect them from damage.

Syntax                 :  cast party shield with <spell>

Valid spells are "iron will" or "aura of force".
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Vor
==============================================================================

<a name="remove vulnerabilities">==============================================================================
Help on spell          :  Remove vulnerabilities
Guild Level            :  Omicron
Spell type             :  Special
Sp Cost                :  350
Casting time           :  10 rounds
Affecting skills       :  master of vulnerability, protection ritual
Affecting stats        :  wis
Base Experience Cost   :  1301
==============================================================================
With this spell, an abjurer may remove harmful affects from a friend (or foe, if
for some reason it was necessary). Keep in mind that this spell will not make
someone more resistant, so much as it will make them less vulnerable. Assuming
the abjurer, friend, or foe have any enchantments which increase
vulnerabilities, the abjurer, should he be familiar with the type of enchantment
used, may attempt to remove said enchantments. It is a slow process, for each
set of enchantments is unique. With that said, there would be very few
enchantments powerful enough to be ignored by the abjurer's efforts.
==============================================================================
Created By             :  Vor
