Evoker Of Flames guild members are taught the knowledge of heat energy and how to shape that energy into mighty spells of fire damage. They learn to cast fiery spells either quickly, efficiently, or widespread. Evokers Of Flames can also focus their amulet to enhance the potency of those spells.</p><pre>

It can be trained up to 25 percent of your skill max at level 2.
It can be trained up to 50 percent of your skill max at level 3.

It can be trained up to 100 percent of your skill max at level 5.
