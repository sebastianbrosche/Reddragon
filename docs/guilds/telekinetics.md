Telekinetic guild members are taught how to use the mind to broaden their senses. They learn to use their mind to sense things that they cannot feel, taste, hear, smell, or see. They learn spells that allow them to sense invisible objects and a foe's combat strength, vulnerabilites, and resistances. They can also sense how large of a weapon their friends can wield.</p><pre>
